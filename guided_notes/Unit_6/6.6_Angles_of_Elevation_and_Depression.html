Class activity: make a clinometer
Work with a partner. Tape a straw along the flat edge of a protractor. Tie a string with a washer to the center hole. Look through the straw at the top of a tree. Read the angle between the string and the 90° mark. That is the angle of elevation. Measure your distance from the tree. Find the height, then add your eye height.
