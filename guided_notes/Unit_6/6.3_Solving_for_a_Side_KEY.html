6.3.3The Unknown Is on the Bottom

  • When x is on the bottom, multiply both sides by x, then divide by the trig value.
  • Check: the hypotenuse must be the longest side.
EX 6. Find x to the nearest tenth.

8 is adjacent. x is the hypotenuse. Use cosine.

cos 25° = 8x, so x = 8cos 25° ≈ 8.8

Divide, do not multiply
x = 8 cos 25° gives 7.3. A leg cannot be longer than the hypotenuse.

6.3.5Real Problems

EX 11. A 24-foot ladder leans against a wall and makes a 75° angle with the ground. How high does it reach? How far is its base from the wall? Nearest tenth.

h = 24 sin 75° ≈ 23.2 ft

base = 24 cos 75° ≈ 6.2 ft

EX 12. A guy wire is anchored 30 feet from the base of a tower and makes a 58° angle with the ground. Find the height h where it is attached and the wire length L. Nearest tenth.

h = 30 tan 58° ≈ 48.0 ft

L = 30cos 58° ≈ 56.6 ft

EX 13. A kite string is 100 feet long and makes a 52° angle with the ground. How high is the kite? Nearest foot.

h = 100 sin 52° ≈ 79 ft

EX 14. A slide is 12 feet long and makes a 38° angle with the ground. How high is the top? How far along the ground does it reach? Nearest tenth.your turn

Height: 12 sin 38° ≈ 7.4 ft. Ground: 12 cos 38° ≈ 9.5 ft.

EX 15. Error analysis. Moses solves cos 25° = 8x and writes x = 8 cos 25° ≈ 7.3. What is wrong?

He multiplied. The unknown is in the denominator, so divide: x = 8cos 25° ≈ 8.8. The hypotenuse must be longer than the leg 8.

Summary
adjacentcosinedividehypotenusemultiplyoppositesinetangent

1. To choose a ratio, name the two sides: the one you know and the one you want. Opposite and hypotenuse mean sine.

2. Adjacent and hypotenuse mean cosine. Opposite and adjacent mean tangent.

3. If the unknown is on top, multiply. If the unknown is on the bottom, divide.

4. The hypotenuse is always the longest side, so use it to check an answer.

Challenge

27. A right triangle has a hypotenuse of 26 and an acute angle of 40°. Find the area of the triangle to the nearest tenth of a square unit.

legs: 26 sin 40° ≈ 16.71 and 26 cos 40° ≈ 19.92. Area = 12(16.71)(19.92) ≈ 166.4.

28. Use sin 30° to find the short leg of a right triangle with hypotenuse 20 and a 30° angle. Compare with the 30°-60°-90° rule from Lesson 6.1.

20 sin 30° = 20 · 0.5 = 10. The rule says the short leg is half the hypotenuse, 10. They agree.
