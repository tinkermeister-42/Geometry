Unit Review - Unit 6: Right Triangle Trigonometry - DHS Geometry
Teacher Key: answers in red

Worked Examples

EX 1. 6.1 A 30-60-90 triangle has a long leg of 9. Find the short leg and the hypotenuse. Give exact answers.

Long leg = s3 = 9, so s = 93 = 933 = 33.

Hypotenuse = 2s = 63 ≈ 10.4.

EX 2. 6.2 A right triangle has legs 8 and 15 and hypotenuse 17. Find sin B, cos B, and tan B for the angle B across from the leg 15. Round to the nearest thousandth.

Opposite = 15, adjacent = 8, hypotenuse = 17.

sin B = 1517 ≈ 0.882, cos B = 817 ≈ 0.471, tan B = 158 = 1.875.

EX 3. 6.3 A ladder leans on a wall. It makes a 68° angle with the ground and its base is 4 feet from the wall. How long is the ladder? Round to the nearest tenth.

The base is adjacent to 68°. The ladder is the hypotenuse. Use cosine.

cos 68° = 4L, so L = 4cos 68° ≈ 10.7 ft.

EX 4. 6.4 A ramp rises 22 inches over a run of 240 inches. Find the angle of the ramp. Round to the nearest tenth of a degree.

Rise is opposite and run is adjacent. Use tangent.

θ = tan−1(22240) ≈ 5.2°.

EX 5. 6.5 Write cos 34° as the sine of an angle. Then find x if sin(x + 4°) = cos(2x − 1°).

cos 34° = sin(90° − 34°) = sin 56°.

The angles add to 90°: (x + 4) + (2x − 1) = 90, so 3x = 87 and x = 29.

EX 6. 6.6 From the top of a 120-foot lighthouse, the angle of depression to a boat is 14°. How far is the boat from the base? Round to the nearest foot.

The angle at the boat is also 14° (alternate interior angles). The height is opposite and the distance is adjacent.

tan 14° = 120d, so d = 120tan 14° ≈ 481 ft.

EX 7. 6.7 A house is 32 feet wide. The roof has a 5/12 pitch and no overhang. Find the roof angle and the rafter length. Round to the nearest tenth.

The run is 16 ft and the rise is 16 · 512 ≈ 6.7 ft.

Angle: tan−1(512) ≈ 22.6°. Rafter: 162 + 6.72 ≈ 17.3 ft.

Practice

6.1 - I can use the 45-45-90 and 30-60-90 triangle patterns

1. A 45-45-90 triangle has a leg of 9 cm. Find the hypotenuse. Give the exact answer and the answer to the nearest tenth.

92 ≈ 12.7 cm

2. A 45-45-90 triangle has a hypotenuse of 18 in. Find each leg. Rationalize and round to the nearest tenth.

182 = 92 ≈ 12.7 in

3. A 30-60-90 triangle has a short leg of 6 m. Find the long leg and the hypotenuse. Round to the nearest tenth.

Long leg 63 ≈ 10.4 m. Hypotenuse 12 m.

4. A 30-60-90 triangle has a hypotenuse of 20 ft. Find both legs. Round to the nearest tenth.

Short leg 10 ft. Long leg 103 ≈ 17.3 ft.

5. A square tile has a diagonal of 30 cm. Find its side. Round to the nearest tenth.

302 = 152 ≈ 21.2 cm

6. Ana says that in a 30-60-90 triangle with a long leg of 9, the hypotenuse is 18. Find her mistake and the correct hypotenuse (nearest tenth).

She doubled the long leg. The hypotenuse is twice the short leg. Short leg = 93 = 33, so the hypotenuse is 63 ≈ 10.4.

6.2 - I can name the sides and write the sine, cosine, and tangent

7. A right triangle has legs 9 and 40 and hypotenuse 41. For the angle B across from the 40, write sin B, cos B, and tan B as fractions.

sin B = 4041, cos B = 941, tan B = 409

8. Use a calculator. Find sin 52°, cos 52°, and tan 52° to the nearest thousandth.

0.788, 0.616, 1.280

9. One right triangle has a 30° angle, a hypotenuse of 10, and an opposite side of 5. Another has a hypotenuse of 24 and an opposite side of 12. Show that sin 30° is the same in both.

510 = 0.5 and 1224 = 0.5. The triangles are similar (AA), so the ratios are equal.

10. A line through the origin makes a 35° angle with the x-axis. Find its slope to the nearest thousandth.

slope = tan 35° ≈ 0.700

11. In a right triangle, tan A = 34. Find the hypotenuse if the legs are 3 and 4, then find sin A and cos A.

Hypotenuse = 5. sin A = 35 = 0.6 and cos A = 45 = 0.8.

12. Ben says sin A = adjacenthypotenuse. Find his mistake.

That is cosine. Sine is opposite over hypotenuse.

6.3 - I can find a side of a right triangle with sine, cosine, or tangent

13. An angle is 38° and the hypotenuse is 25. Find the opposite side. Round to the nearest tenth.

25 sin 38° ≈ 15.4

14. An angle is 55° and the adjacent side is 14. Find the hypotenuse. Round to the nearest tenth.

14cos 55° ≈ 24.4

15. An angle is 27° and the adjacent side is 60. Find the opposite side. Round to the nearest tenth.

60 tan 27° ≈ 30.6

16. A kite string is 80 feet long and makes a 62° angle with the ground. How high is the kite? Ignore sag. Round to the nearest tenth.

80 sin 62° ≈ 70.6 ft

17. A 5-meter ladder makes a 72° angle with the ground. How far is its base from the wall? Round to the nearest tenth.

5 cos 72° ≈ 1.5 m

18. Dara solves tan 40° = 9x and writes x = 9 tan 40° ≈ 7.6. Find her mistake and the correct x (nearest tenth).

x is on the bottom, so divide: x = 9tan 40° ≈ 10.7.

6.4 - I can find an angle of a right triangle with an inverse ratio

19. Find A if sin A = 0.6. Round to the nearest tenth of a degree.

A = sin−1(0.6) ≈ 36.9°

20. A right triangle has legs 9 and 14. Find the angle across from the 9. Round to the nearest tenth of a degree.

tan−1(914) ≈ 32.7°

21. The hypotenuse is 30 and the adjacent side is 24. Find both acute angles. Round to the nearest tenth of a degree.

cos−1(0.8) ≈ 36.9° and 90° − 36.9° = 53.1°

22. A ramp rises 20 inches over a run of 200 inches. Find the angle of the ramp. Round to the nearest tenth of a degree.

tan−1(0.1) ≈ 5.7°

23. A stair has a riser of 7 inches and a tread of 11 inches. Find the angle of the stairs. Round to the nearest tenth of a degree.

tan−1(711) ≈ 32.5°

24. A(1, 2) and B(9, 7) are on a line. Find the angle the line makes with the x-axis. Round to the nearest tenth of a degree.

slope = 58, so tan−1(58) ≈ 32.0°

6.5 - I can use sine and cosine of complementary angles

25. Write cos 20° as the sine of an angle.

sin 70°

26. sin 28° ≈ 0.469. What is cos 62° to the nearest thousandth?

62° = 90° − 28°, so cos 62° = sin 28° ≈ 0.469

27. tan 18° ≈ 0.325. Use this to find tan 72° to the nearest hundredth.

tan 72° = 1tan 18° ≈ 3.08

28. Solve sin x = cos(2x + 9°).

x + 2x + 9 = 90, so 3x = 81 and x = 27.

29. In a right triangle, sin A = 725. What is cos B for the other acute angle B?

A and B are complementary, so cos B = sin A = 725.

30. Lia says sin 40° = cos 40°. Give a counterexample with numbers.

sin 40° ≈ 0.643 and cos 40° ≈ 0.766. They are not equal.

6.6 - I can solve problems with angles of elevation and depression

31. You stand 60 m from a building. Your eyes are 1.6 m above the ground. The angle of elevation to the top is 48°. How tall is the building? Round to the nearest tenth.

60 tan 48° + 1.6 ≈ 68.2 m

32. From the top of an 85-foot cliff, the angle of depression to a boat is 33°. How far is the boat from the base of the cliff? Round to the nearest tenth.

85tan 33° ≈ 130.9 ft

33. A plane is 2000 feet above the ground. The angle of depression to the end of the runway is 4°. Find the ground distance to the nearest foot.

2000tan 4° ≈ 28601 ft

34. You stand 120 feet from a building. The angle of elevation to the bottom of a flagpole on the roof is 25°. To the top it is 31°. How tall is the flagpole? Ignore your eye height. Round to the nearest tenth.

120(tan 31° − tan 25°) ≈ 16.1 ft

35. A student says the angle of depression from a tower to a car is 55°, so the angle of elevation from the car to the tower is 35°. Find the mistake.

They are equal (alternate interior angles). The angle of elevation is 55°.

6.7 - I can solve real problems with right triangles

36. A porch is 21 inches high. A ramp follows the 1-to-12 rule. Find the run in feet and the ramp length in feet. Round to the nearest tenth.

Run = 12 · 21 = 252 in = 21 ft. Length = 212 + 2522 ≈ 252.9 in ≈ 21.1 ft.

37. A house is 36 feet wide. The roof has a 5/12 pitch and no overhang. Find the roof angle and the rafter length. Round to the nearest tenth.

Run 18, rise 7.5. Angle tan−1(512) ≈ 22.6°. Rafter 182 + 7.52 ≈ 19.5 ft.

38. A boat sails 15 km on a bearing of 040°. How far north and how far east of its start is it? Round to the nearest tenth.

North: 15 cos 40° ≈ 11.5 km. East: 15 sin 40° ≈ 9.6 km.

39. A drone hovers 50 m above the ground between two markers. The angles of depression to the markers are 35° and 55°. How far apart are the markers? Round to the nearest tenth.

50tan 35° + 50tan 55° ≈ 71.41 + 35.01 ≈ 106.4 m

40. A 12-foot ladder is set so that its base is 1 foot from the wall for every 4 feet of height. How high does it reach? How far is its base from the wall? Round to the nearest tenth.

The angle is tan−1(4) ≈ 76.0°. Height: 12 sin 76.0° ≈ 11.6 ft. Base: 12 cos 76.0° ≈ 2.9 ft.
