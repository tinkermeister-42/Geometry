5.1.1Ratios

  • A ratio compares two numbers by division.
  • Three ways to write a ratio of 2 to 3: 2 to 3, 2 : 3, and 23.
  • To simplify a ratio, divide both numbers by the same number.
  • Both quantities must use the same units before you write the ratio.
Order matters. The ratio of juice to water is 2 : 3. The ratio of water to juice is 3 : 2. These are different. The first number goes with the first thing named.
EX 1. Write and simplify a ratio. A box has 15 red tiles and 25 blue tiles.
a. Write the ratio of red tiles to blue tiles.
b. Write the ratio of blue tiles to all the tiles.

a. 15 : 25. Divide both by 5. The ratio is 3 : 5.

b. There are 15 + 25 = 40 tiles. The ratio is 25 : 40. Divide both by 5. The ratio is 5 : 8.

EX 2. a. Write the ratio of tablets to laptops in lowest terms.
b. Write the ratio of laptops to all the devices in lowest terms.your turn

a. 30 : 18 = 5 : 3

b. 18 : 48 = 3 : 8

EX 3. Ratios with units. Ahmad has a board that is 2 feet long. He cuts a piece that is 8 inches long. Write the ratio of the board to the piece in lowest terms.

Change feet to inches: 2  ft = 24  in.

The ratio is 24 : 8. Divide both by 8. The ratio is 3 : 1.

The board is 3 times as long as the piece.

EX 4. What is the ratio of 3 yards to 6 feet?your turn

3  yd = 9  ft. The ratio is 9 : 6 = 3 : 2.

5.1.2Proportions

  • A proportion is an equation that says two ratios are equal.
  • In the proportion ab = cd, the cross products are a · d and b · c.
  • Cross products are equal: if ab = cd, then ad = bc.
  • Put the same kind of number on top in both ratios, such as juice on top.
EX 5. Solve a proportion. Solve x12 = 58.

Cross products: 8 · x = 12 · 5.

8x = 60

x = 7.5

Check: 7.512 = 0.625 and 58 = 0.625.

EX 6. x10 = 74your turn

EX 7. A proportion from a word problem. Moses walks 6 miles in 2 hours. He keeps the same speed. How long does it take him to walk 15 miles?

Put miles on top and hours on the bottom in both ratios. Let t be the hours.

62 = 15t

6t = 2 · 15 = 30

t = 5

It takes 5 hours.

EX 8. A printer prints 45 pages in 6 minutes. How many pages does it print in 20 minutes?your turn

456 = p20, so 6p = 900 and p = 150 pages.

EX 9. Expressions in a proportion. Solve 5x + 2 = 4x.

Cross products: 5 · x = 4(x + 2).

5x = 4x + 8

x = 8

Check: 510 = 0.5 and 48 = 0.5.

EX 10. 7x = 3x − 4your turn

7(x − 4) = 3x, so 7x − 28 = 3x and 4x = 28. x = 7.

EX 11. A proportion with two answers. Solve x − 25 = 20x − 2.

Cross products: (x − 2)(x − 2) = 5 · 20.

(x − 2)2 = 100

x − 2 = 10 or x − 2 = −10

x = 12 or x = −8

Both work in the equation. In a figure, a length cannot be negative, so you would keep only x = 12.

EX 12. x + 14 = 9x + 1your turn

(x + 1)2 = 36, so x + 1 = 6 or x + 1 = −6. x = 5 or x = −7.

5.1.3Scale Drawings and Maps

  • A scale drawing has the same shape as the real thing but a different size.
  • The scale tells how a length on the drawing compares to the real length, such as 1  in : 20  ft.
  • Real length = drawing length × 20 when the scale is 1  in : 20  ft.
  • To find a drawing length, divide the real length by the scale number.
EX 13. Use a scale. The plan of a lot has the scale 1  in : 20  ft. On the plan, the lot is 3.5 in long and 2 in wide.
a. Find the real length of the lot.
b. A shed is 12 ft wide. How wide is the shed on the plan?

a. Let L be the real length in feet.

120 = 3.5L, so L = 20 · 3.5 = 70 ft.

The lot is 70 ft long (and 2 · 20 = 40 ft wide).

b. Let d be the width on the plan in inches.

120 = d12, so 20d = 12 and d = 0.6.

The shed is 0.6 in wide on the plan.

EX 14. a. Two towns are 3.5 in apart on the map. Find the real distance.
b. Two lakes are 50 mi apart. How far apart are they on the map?your turn

a. 3.5 · 8 = 28 mi.

b. 18 = d50, so 8d = 50 and d = 6.25 in.

5.1.4Ratios in a Figure

  • When a ratio is 2 : 3 : 4, call the parts 2x, 3x, and 4x.
  • The angles of a triangle add to 180°.
  • The parts of a segment add to the total length.
  • Add the parts, solve for x, then multiply to find each part.
EX 15. Angles in a ratio. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find each angle.

The angles add to 180°.

2x + 3x + 4x = 180

9x = 180, so x = 20.

The angles are 2(20) = 40°, 3(20) = 60°, and 4(20) = 80°.

Check: 40 + 60 + 80 = 180.

EX 16. Find each angle. Then say whether the triangle is acute, right, or obtuse.your turn

x + 2x + 6x = 180, so 9x = 180 and x = 20.

The angles are 20°, 40°, and 120°. The triangle is obtuse.

EX 17. A segment in a ratio. Point P is on AB. AB = 40 in. The ratio AP : PB is 3 : 5. Find AP and PB.

AP + PB = AB

3x + 5x = 40

8x = 40, so x = 5.

AP = 3(5) = 15 in and PB = 5(5) = 25 in.

EX 18. A mix is cement : sand : gravel = 1 : 2 : 3 by volume. The total is 27 cubic feet. How much of each is there?your turn

x + 2x + 3x = 27, so 6x = 27 and x = 4.5.

Cement: 4.5 cubic ft. Sand: 9 cubic ft. Gravel: 13.5 cubic ft.

Summary
5xcross productsproportionratioscale

1. A ratio compares two numbers by division.

2. Two ratios that are equal make a proportion.

3. In a proportion, the cross products are equal: ad = bc.

4. A scale of 1  in : 20  ft means 1 inch on the drawing is 20 feet in real life.

5. To split a total in the ratio 3 : 5, write the parts as 3x and 5x, then add them to the total.

16. Thu solves 3x = 520. She writes 3 · 5 = 20x and gets x = 0.75. Find her mistake. Then solve the proportion correctly.

She multiplied across the top and across the bottom. Cross products go along the diagonals: 3 · 20 = 5 · x. So 60 = 5x and x = 12.

7x = 28, so x = 4. Gravel: 4 · 4 = 16 cubic ft.

The angles add to 360°. 12x = 360, so x = 30. The angles are 60°, 90°, 90°, and 120°.

Challenge

29. A map has the scale 1  in : 20  mi. A park is a rectangle 2 in by 3 in on the map. Find the real area of the park in square miles. Hint: do not multiply the map area by 20.

The real sides are 2 · 20 = 40 mi and 3 · 20 = 60 mi. The area is 40 · 60 = 2400 square miles. (The map area is 6 square inches, and 6 · 20 = 120 is wrong.)
