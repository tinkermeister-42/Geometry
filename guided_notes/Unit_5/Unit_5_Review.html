Worked Examples

b. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. Find each angle.

Practice

5.1 - I can write and solve proportions, use scales, and split a total in a ratio

1. Write the ratio of 24 inches to 4 feet in lowest terms.

2. Solve 7x = 149.

3. Solve x + 49 = x6.

4. A map has the scale 1  cm : 6  km. Two towns are 8.5 cm apart on the map. How far apart are they?

5. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 2 : 5. Find each angle.

6. Amira solves 4x = 615. She writes 4 · 6 = 15x and gets x = 1.6. Find her mistake and solve it correctly.

5.2 - I can decide whether figures are similar and use the scale factor

7. Are a 5 in by 8 in photo and a 15 in by 24 in photo similar? If so, give k.

8. △ ABC ∼ △ PQR. Which angle matches ∠B? Which side matches AC?

9. △ ABC ∼ △ DEF. AB = 14, DE = 21, and BC = 10. Find EF.

10. The scale factor from a large figure to a small figure is 13. The large figure has area 63 and perimeter 45. Find the area and perimeter of the small figure.

11. Mohammed says any two rhombuses are similar because all their sides are equal. Give a counterexample.

5.3 - I can decide whether two triangles are similar and find missing sides

12. △ ABC: m∠A = 40°, m∠B = 75°. △ DEF: m∠D = 40°, m∠F = 65°. Are they similar? Write the statement.

13. Triangle 1 has sides 6, 10, 14. Triangle 2 has sides 9, 15, 21. Are they similar?

14. AB = 4, AC = 6, m∠A = 30°. DE = 6, DF = 10, m∠D = 30°. Are the triangles similar?

15. DE ∥ BC, AD = 6, DB = 3, and DE = 10. Find BC.

16. A(0, 0), B(3, 0), C(0, 4) and D(1, 1), E(7, 1), F(1, 9). Use distances to show the triangles are similar. Find k.

5.4 - I can use parallel lines and midsegments in triangles

17. DE ∥ BC, AD = 8, DB = 12, and AE = 10. Find EC.

18. AD = 4, DB = 6, AE = 6, and EC = 9. Is DE ∥ BC?

19. A midsegment is 11 cm long. How long is the side it is parallel to?

20. A midsegment is 3x + 2 long. The side it is parallel to is 8x − 6 long. Find x and the midsegment.

21. Three parallel lines cut two transversals. On one, the parts are 8 and 12. On the other, the first part is 10. Find the second part.

5.5 - I can use similar triangles to measure heights and distances

22. A 1.5 m stick has a shadow 2 m long. A tower casts a shadow 50 m long at the same time. How tall is the tower?

23. Eye height 1.6 m. The person is 2.4 m from a mirror. A tree is 18 m from the mirror. How tall is the tree?

24. River stakes: BC = 18 m, CD = 6 m, and DE = 7 m. Find the width AB.

25. A person 5 ft tall stands 8 ft from a lamp. Her shadow is 4 ft long. How tall is the lamp?
