5.5  Indirect Measurement

Guided Notes - Unit 5: Similarity - DHS Geometry
Teacher Key: answers in red
Objectives
  • Use shadows to find a height
  • Use a mirror to find a height
  • Find a distance across a river with stakes
  • Set up and solve real problems with similar triangles

Warm-Up

1. Solve 64 = h30.

4h = 180, so h = 45

2. Two right triangles each have a 35° angle. Are they similar? Say why.

Yes. Each has a right angle and a 35° angle. That is AA.

3. Change 5 ft 6 in to feet.

6  in = 0.5  ft, so 5.5 ft.

5.5.1Shadows

  • The sun is far away. Its rays are parallel where they reach the ground.
  • A person and a tree make two right triangles with the same sun angle.
  • The triangles are similar by AA.
  • Keep the same kind of length in the same place: height over shadow on both sides.
Four steps for every problem. 1. Draw a sketch and label it. 2. Name the similar triangles. 3. Write a proportion with matching sides in the same positions. 4. Solve, and answer with units.
EX 1. Height of a tree. A person 5 ft tall has a shadow 8 ft long. At the same time, a tree has a shadow 40 ft long. How tall is the tree?

The triangles are similar. Put height over shadow on both sides.

58 = h40

8h = 200, so h = 25.

The tree is 25 ft tall.

EX 2. A stick 3 ft tall casts a shadow 2 ft long. At the same time, a pole casts a shadow 18 ft long. How tall is the pole?your turn

32 = h18, so 2h = 54 and h = 27 ft.

5.5.2Mirrors

  • A mirror reflects light so that the angle in equals the angle out.
  • The person and the building both stand straight up, so each triangle has a right angle.
  • The triangles are similar by AA.
  • Use the eye height, not the full height of the person.
EX 3. Height of a building. Mei's eyes are 1.6 m above the ground. She stands 2 m from a mirror on the ground and sees the top of a building in it. The mirror is 30 m from the building. How tall is the building?

The small triangle has legs 1.6 and 2. The large triangle has legs h and 30.

1.62 = h30

2h = 48, so h = 24.

The building is 24 m tall.

EX 4. Daniel's eyes are 1.5 m above the ground. He stands 3 m from a mirror and sees the top of a tree. The mirror is 36 m from the tree. How tall is the tree?your turn

1.53 = h36, so 3h = 54 and h = 18 m.

5.5.3Across a River

  • Put stakes B, C, and D in a line on your side of the river.
  • Walk from D away from the river to E, where you see C and the tree A in a line. Then △ ABC and △ EDC are similar.
  • Match the sides: ABDE = BCCD.
EX 5. Width of a river. BC = 20 m, CD = 5 m, and DE = 6 m. Find the width AB of the river.

△ ABC ∼ △ EDC. Match AB with ED and BC with DC.

ABDE = BCCD, so x6 = 205.

5x = 120, so x = 24.

The river is 24 m wide.

EX 6. BC = 15 m, CD = 5 m, and DE = 4 m. Find AB.your turn

x4 = 155, so 5x = 60 and x = 12 m.

5.5.4Mixed Real Problems

  • Read the problem and make a sketch first.
  • Decide which lengths are the sides of the big triangle. Sometimes the base is two lengths added together.
  • Write the proportion, solve, and give the answer with units.
EX 7. Which lengths match?. A lamp stands on a level street. Tomás is 6 ft tall. He stands 10 ft from the base of the lamp. His shadow is 5 ft long. How tall is the lamp?

The small triangle: height 6, base 5 (the shadow).

The big triangle: height h, base 10 + 5 = 15 (from the lamp to the tip of the shadow).

65 = h15

5h = 90, so h = 18.

The lamp is 18 ft tall.

EX 8. Maria is 5 ft tall. She stands 12 ft from a lamp post. Her shadow is 6 ft long. How tall is the lamp post?your turn

Big base: 12 + 6 = 18. 56 = h18, so 6h = 90 and h = 15 ft.

EX 9. Test wording. A vertical post that is 2.5 feet tall casts a shadow that is 1.5 feet long. At the same time, a nearby tower casts a shadow that is 36 feet long. What is the height of the tower?
A. 21.6 ft B. 40 ft C. 60 ft D. 100 ft

Draw the sketch. Height over shadow:

2.51.5 = h36, so 1.5h = 90 and h = 60.

The answer is C, 60 ft.

Class activity. Find the height of the school flagpole. Work with a partner. Measure your height, your shadow, and the flagpole's shadow with a tape measure. Write a proportion and solve. Then check with a mirror on the ground.
Summary
outsamesimilarsketch

1. Indirect measurement uses similar triangles to find a length you cannot measure directly.

2. A person and a tree at the same time make similar triangles because the sun angle is the same.

3. In a mirror problem, the angle in equals the angle out, so the triangles are similar by AA.

4. For a river, the triangles ABC and EDC are similar, so ABDE = BCCD.

5. Always draw a sketch and give the answer with units.

5.5 Indirect Measurement - Practice

Practice

Shadows

1. A girl 5 ft tall casts a shadow 7.5 ft long. At the same time a tree casts a shadow 36 ft long. How tall is the tree?

57.5 = h36, so 7.5h = 180 and h = 24 ft

2. A pole casts a shadow 21 ft long. At the same time a 3 ft stick casts a shadow 2 ft long. How tall is the pole?

32 = h21, so 2h = 63 and h = 31.5 ft

3. Hnin is 1.6 m tall. Her shadow is 2.4 m long. A building casts a shadow 45 m long at the same time. How tall is the building?

1.62.4 = h45, so 2.4h = 72 and h = 30 m

4. A boy is 4 ft 6 in tall. His shadow is 6 ft long. A tree casts a shadow 40 ft long. How tall is the tree? Change inches to feet first.

4  ft  6  in = 4.5 ft. 4.56 = h40, so 6h = 180 and h = 30 ft

5. A flagpole is 30 ft tall. Its shadow is 24 ft long. At the same time, a person 5 ft tall stands next to it. How long is the person's shadow?

3024 = 5s, so 30s = 120 and s = 4 ft

6. A sign post 6 ft tall has a shadow 5 ft long. A building casts a shadow 90 ft long at the same time. How tall is the building?

65 = h90, so 5h = 540 and h = 108 ft

Mirrors

7. Eye height 1.5 m. The person stands 2 m from a mirror. The building is 40 m from the mirror. How tall is the building?

1.52 = h40, so 2h = 60 and h = 30 m

8. A person with eye height 5 ft stands 3 ft from a mirror. A tree is 24 ft from the mirror on the other side. How tall is the tree?

53 = h24, so 3h = 120 and h = 40 ft

9. Samir is 1.8 m tall. His eyes are 1.7 m above the ground. He stands 2.5 m from a mirror. The tree is 20 m from the mirror. How tall is the tree? Which of his heights do you use?

Use the eye height, 1.7 m. 1.72.5 = h20, so 2.5h = 34 and h = 13.6 m

10. Explain why the two triangles in a mirror problem are similar.

Both are right triangles because the person and the building stand straight up. The angle where the light hits the mirror equals the angle where it leaves. Two pairs of equal angles means similar by AA.

Across a river. Use the stake method from 5.5.3. △ ABC ∼ △ EDC

11. BC = 30 ft, CD = 10 ft, and DE = 14 ft. Find the width AB.

x14 = 3010, so 10x = 420 and x = 42 ft

12. BC = 24 m, CD = 6 m, and DE = 5 m. Find the width AB.

x5 = 246, so 6x = 120 and x = 20 m

Mixed problems

13. A tree casts a shadow (2x + 6) ft long. The tree is (4x + 3) ft tall. At the same time a 6 ft person casts a shadow 4 ft long. Find x and the height of the tree.

4x + 32x + 6 = 64, so 4(4x + 3) = 6(2x + 6). 16x + 12 = 12x + 36, so 4x = 24 and x = 6. The tree is 4(6) + 3 = 27 ft tall.

14. A lamp post is 20 ft tall. Ana is 5 ft tall and stands 15 ft from the post. How long is her shadow? Let s be the length of her shadow.

The big triangle has base 15 + s. 2015 + s = 5s, so 20s = 5(15 + s). 20s = 75 + 5s, so 15s = 75 and s = 5 ft.

15. A ramp rises 3 ft over a ground length of 36 ft. A support post stands 24 ft from the bottom, measured along the ground. How tall is the post?

336 = h24, so 36h = 72 and h = 2 ft

16. Kofi is 4 ft tall. He stands 9 ft from a lamp. His shadow is 3 ft long. He writes 43 = H9 and gets H = 12 ft. Find his mistake. Then find the height of the lamp.

The big triangle's base goes from the lamp to the tip of the shadow, 9 + 3 = 12 ft, not 9. Correct: 43 = H12, so 3H = 48 and H = 16 ft.

17. A 2 m stick casts a shadow 1.5 m long. How long is the shadow of a 9 m tower at the same time?

21.5 = 9s, so 2s = 13.5 and s = 6.75 m

A. 2.530 = h1.5

A. 2.7 m

B. 16 m

Challenge

20. At 3:00 a 6 ft person has a shadow 9 ft long. A tree has a shadow 54 ft long. Find the height of the tree. Later the person's shadow is 12 ft long. How long is the tree's shadow then?

69 = h54, so 9h = 324 and h = 36 ft. Later: 612 = 36s, so 6s = 432 and s = 72 ft. The tree's shadow is always 6 times the person's shadow.
