2.3.1Squaring a Corner

  • Mark 3 and 4 on the edges. If the marks are 5 apart, the corner is square.
  • The diagonal of a rectangle is the hypotenuse of a right triangle whose legs are the sides.
  • If the diagonal is not the length the theorem gives, the corner is not square.
EX 1. A deck is 10 ft by 24 ft. Kofi measures the diagonal and gets 26 ft 2 in. Is the deck square?

Draw the rectangle. The diagonal and two sides make a right triangle.

The diagonal should be c = 102 + 242 = 676 = 26 ft.

Kofi measured 26 ft 2 in, which is 2 inches too long. The deck is not square. The corner needs to be pushed in.

EX 2. A slab is 15 ft by 20 ft. How long should its diagonal be if the slab is square?your turn

c = 152 + 202 = 625 = 25 ft. (It is 5 · (3, 4, 5).)

2.3.2Ladders

  • The ladder is the hypotenuse. The wall and the ground are the legs.
  • 4-to-1 rule: base distance ≥ height ÷ 4.
EX 3. A 20 ft ladder has its base 5 ft from a wall. How high does it reach? Does it follow the 4-to-1 rule? Round to the nearest tenth.

52 + h2 = 202  so  h2 = 375

h = 375 = 515 ≈ 19.4 ft.

Rule: 19.4 ÷ 4 ≈ 4.9. The base needs to be at least 4.9 ft out. It is 5 ft out, so the ladder follows the rule.

EX 4. A 24 ft ladder has its base 6 ft from a wall. How high does it reach? Round to the nearest tenth.your turn

h = 242 − 62 = 540 = 615 ≈ 23.2 ft.

2.3.3Screens

  • The screen size is the length of the diagonal.
  • The width and height are the legs. The diagonal is the hypotenuse.
EX 5. A TV screen is 40 in wide and 22.5 in tall. What is its screen size? Round to the nearest tenth.

d2 = 402 + 22.52 = 1600 + 506.25 = 2106.25

d = 2106.25 ≈ 45.9. It is a 45.9 inch screen (sold as a 46 inch TV).

EX 6. A monitor has a 24 in diagonal and is 12 in tall. How wide is it? Round to the nearest tenth.your turn

w = 242 − 122 = 432 = 123 ≈ 20.8 in.

2.3.4Shortcuts and Distances

  • A shortcut across a rectangle is a diagonal.
  • To compare, find the diagonal and the distance around the sides.
EX 7. A park is 300 ft by 400 ft. Moses walks from one corner to the opposite corner. How much shorter is the diagonal path than walking along two sides?

Along two sides: 300 + 400 = 700 ft.

Diagonal: 3002 + 4002 = 250000 = 500 ft. (It is 100 · (3, 4, 5).)

700 − 500 = 200. The diagonal path is 200 ft shorter.

EX 8. A baseball diamond is a square with 90 ft between bases. How far is it from home plate to second base? Round to the nearest tenth.

Home to first and first to second are the legs. Home to second is the diagonal.

d = 902 + 902 = 16200 = 902 ≈ 127.3 ft.

EX 9. A ramp rises 3 ft over a ground distance of 36 ft. How long is the ramp? Round to the nearest tenth.your turn

362 + 32 = 1305 ≈ 36.1 ft.

2.3.5Two Steps: Boxes

  • Step 1: the floor diagonal is length2 + width2.
  • Step 2: the pole is (floor diagonal)2 + height2.
EX 10. A box is 12 in long, 9 in wide, and 8 in tall. Find the longest pole that fits.

Step 1, the floor: f = 122 + 92 = 225 = 15 in.

Step 2, the pole: p = 152 + 82 = 289 = 17 in.

The longest pole is 17 in.

EX 11. A closet is 4 ft by 4 ft and 7 ft tall. How long is the longest rod that fits?your turn

Floor: 16 + 16 = 32. Rod: 32 + 49 = 81 = 9 ft.

Summary
diagonalhypotenusedrawunits4-to-1two

1. Start every word problem with a drawing of the right triangle.

2. A ladder, a screen, and a shortcut are each a hypotenuse or a leg.

3. A TV size is the length of its diagonal.

4. The 4-to-1 rule: 1 foot out for every 4 feet up.

5. A box needs two right triangles. End the answer with units.

22. A 10 ft ladder leans on a wall with its base 6 ft from the wall. The top slides down 1 ft. How far does the base move out? Round to the nearest tenth.
