8.5.1Pyramids and Cones

  • Pour a pyramid into a prism with the same base and height. It takes 3 pours to fill the prism.
  • Pyramid: V = 13Bh
  • Cone: V = 13πr2h
  • h is the straight-up height, not the slant height.
EX 2. A square pyramid has base side 6 ft and height 8 ft. Find the volume.

B = 6 · 6 = 36

V = 13(36)(8) = 96 cubic feet

EX 3. A pyramid has a rectangular base 5 ft by 8 ft and height 6 ft. Find the volume.your turn

B = 40. V = 13(40)(6) = 80 cubic feet.

EX 4. A cone has radius 4 in and height 9 in. Find the volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

V = 13π(4)2(9) = 48 π

≈ 150.8 cubic inches

EX 5. A cone has radius 3 cm and height 7 cm. Find the volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.your turn

V = 13π(9)(7) = 21 π ≈ 66.0 cubic centimeters.

8.5.2Finding the Height First

The height, half the base (or the radius), and the slant height make a right triangle. Use the Pythagorean theorem to find h. Then find the volume.
EX 6. A square pyramid has base side 10 in and slant height 13 in. Find the volume.

Half the base = 5

h2 = 132 − 52 = 144, so h = 12

V = 13(100)(12) = 400 cubic inches

EX 7. A square pyramid has base side 18 cm and slant height 15 cm. Find the volume.your turn

Half the base = 9. h2 = 225 − 81 = 144, h = 12.

V = 13(324)(12) = 1296 cubic centimeters.

EX 8. A cone has radius 6 in and slant height 10 in. Find the volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

h2 = 102 − 62 = 64, so h = 8

V = 13π(36)(8) = 96 π

≈ 301.6 cubic inches

EX 9. A cone has radius 9 ft and slant height 15 ft. Find the volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.your turn

h2 = 225 − 81 = 144, h = 12. V = 13π(81)(12) = 324 π ≈ 1017.9 cubic feet.

8.5.3Spheres and Hemispheres

  • Sphere: V = 43πr3
  • Hemisphere: V = 23πr3
  • The radius is cubed, not squared.
EX 10. Find the volume of a sphere with radius 6 cm. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

V = 43π(6)3 = 43π(216) = 288 π ≈ 904.8 cubic centimeters

EX 11. Find the volume of a sphere with radius 9 in. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.your turn

V = 43π(729) = 972 π ≈ 3053.6 cubic inches

EX 12. A bowl is a hemisphere with radius 5 in. How much water does it hold when full? Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

V = 23π(5)3

= 2503π ≈ 261.8 cubic inches

EX 13. A dome is a hemisphere with radius 12 ft. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.your turn

V = 23π(1728) = 1152 π ≈ 3619.1 cubic feet

EX 14. A sphere has volume 36 π cubic inches. Find its radius.

43πr3 = 36 π, so r3 = 36 · 34 = 27, so r = 3 in

EX 15. A sphere has volume 5003π cubic centimeters. Find its radius.your turn

4r3 = 500, r3 = 125, r = 5 cm

8.5.4Composite Solids

Break the solid into pieces you know. Find each volume. Add the pieces, or subtract a piece that was removed.
EX 16. A silo is a cylinder with radius 6 ft and height 20 ft, with a hemisphere on top. Find the volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

Cylinder: π(6)2(20) = 720 π

Hemisphere: 23π(6)3 = 144 π

V = 864 π ≈ 2714.3 cubic feet

EX 17. A capsule is a cylinder with radius 2 cm and height 6 cm, with a hemisphere on each end. Find the volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.your turn

Cylinder: π(4)(6) = 24 π. Two hemispheres = one sphere: 43π(8) = 323π.

V = 1043π ≈ 108.9 cubic centimeters

EX 18. A cone has radius 3 cm and height 10 cm. A hemisphere with radius 3 cm sits on top. Find the total volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

Cone: 13π(9)(10) = 30 π

Hemisphere: 23π(27) = 18 π

V = 48 π ≈ 150.8 cubic centimeters

EX 19. A cone has radius 2 cm and height 8 cm. A hemisphere with radius 2 cm sits on top. Find the volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.your turn

Cone: 13π(4)(8) = 323π. Hemisphere: 23π(8) = 163π. Total: 16 π ≈ 50.3 cubic centimeters.

EX 20. A funnel is a cone with the tip cut off. The whole cone has radius 6 ft and height 12 ft. The tip that was cut off has radius 3 ft and height 6 ft. Find the volume of the funnel. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

Whole cone: 13π(36)(12) = 144 π

Tip: 13π(9)(6) = 18 π

V = 126 π ≈ 395.8 cubic feet

EX 21. Find the mistake. Ana says a cone with radius 3 and height 5 has volume π(3)2(5) = 45 π. What is the correct volume?your turn

She forgot the 13. V = 13π(9)(5) = 15 π ≈ 47.1.

8.5.5Real Problems

EX 22. A pile of sand is a cone. The diameter is 12 ft and the height is 5 ft. How many cubic yards is it? Round to the nearest tenth.

r = 6   V = 13π(36)(5) = 60 π ≈ 188.5 cubic feet

188.5 ÷ 27 ≈ 7.0 cubic yards

EX 23. A pile of salt is a cone with radius 8 ft and height 6 ft. How many cubic yards is it? Round to the nearest tenth.your turn

V = 13π(64)(6) = 128 π ≈ 402.1 cubic feet. 402.1 ÷ 27 ≈ 14.9 cubic yards.

Summary
addcubedone thirdPythagorean theoremsubtract

1. A pyramid or cone holds one third of the prism or cylinder with the same base and height.

2. For a sphere, the radius is cubed: V = 43πr3.

3. Find the height from the slant height with the Pythagorean theorem.

4. For composite solids, add pieces or subtract a removed piece.

Composite Solids

Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth when there is a π.

18. A silo is a cylinder with radius 5 ft and height 12 ft, with a hemisphere on top.

300 π + 2503π = 11503π ≈ 1204.3 cubic feet

19. An ice cream cone has radius 4 cm and height 6 cm. A scoop shaped like a hemisphere with radius 4 cm sits on top. Find the total volume.

13π(16)(6) = 32 π. 23π(64) = 1283π. Total 2243π ≈ 234.6 cubic centimeters

20. A cone with radius 10 cm and height 15 cm has its top cut off. The tip that was removed has radius 4 cm and height 6 cm. Find the volume that is left.

500 π − 32 π = 468 π ≈ 1470.3 cubic centimeters

216 + 13(36)(4) = 216 + 48 = 264 cubic inches

144 π − 48 π = 96 π ≈ 301.6 cubic meters

Real Problems

23. A pile of sand is a cone with diameter 10 ft and height 4 ft. How many cubic yards is it? Round to the nearest tenth.

13π(25)(4) ≈ 104.7 cubic feet. 104.7 ÷ 27 ≈ 3.9 cubic yards

24. A pile of road salt is a cone with radius 7 ft and height 5 ft. A truck carries 4 cubic yards. How many truck loads are needed to move the pile?

13π(49)(5) ≈ 256.6 ft3 ÷ 27 ≈ 9.5 yd3. 9.5 ÷ 4 ≈ 2.4, so 3 loads

25. A coffee filter is a cone with radius 5 cm and height 12 cm. How many milliliters of water does it hold when full? (1 cm3 = 1 mL.) Round to the nearest milliliter.

13π(25)(12) = 100 π ≈ 314 mL

26. A beach ball has radius 30 cm. How many liters of air does it hold? (1000 cm3 = 1 liter.) Round to the nearest tenth.

43π(27,000) = 36,000 π ≈ 113,097 cm3 ≈ 113.1 liters

Challenge

29. A cylinder has radius r and height r. A cone with the same radius and height is cut out. Write the volume that is left as a multiple of πr3. What solid has the same volume?

πr3 − 13πr3 = 23πr3. A hemisphere with radius r.

30. A sphere has radius 3. Another sphere has radius 6. How many times larger is the volume of the larger sphere? Check by finding both volumes.

Small: 43π(27) = 36 π. Large: 43π(216) = 288 π. 288 ÷ 36 = 8 times larger.
