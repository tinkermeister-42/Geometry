Unit 8 Review - PracticeName

Practice

8.1 - I can find the area of polygons and composite figures.

1. A parallelogram has a base of 14 cm and a height of 9 cm. Find its area.

2. A triangle has vertices (1, 1), (9, 1) and (5, 7). Find its area in square units.

3. A trapezoid has bases 11 in and 17 in and a height of 8 in. Find its area.

4. A regular hexagon has sides of 4 m and an apothem of about 3.46 m. Find its area to the nearest tenth.

5. A wall is 20 ft wide and 9 ft tall. It has a door 3 ft by 7 ft and a window 4 ft by 3 ft. One gallon of paint covers 350 ft2. How much wall must be painted? How many whole gallons are needed?

6. A triangle has an area of 54 in2 and a base of 12 in. Find its height.

8.2 - I can name solids, describe cross sections, and spin shapes into solids.

7. A pentagonal prism has 7 faces and 10 vertices. How many edges does it have? Check with F + V − E = 2.

8. A solid has 6 faces and 8 vertices. How many edges does it have?

9. A plane cuts a square pyramid parallel to its base. What shape is the cross section?

10. Name the cross section: (a) a plane through the axis of a cylinder, (b) any plane through the center of a sphere.

11. A rectangle 4 cm wide and 7 cm tall spins about its 7 cm side. Name the solid. Give its radius and height.

12. A right triangle with legs 5 and 12 spins about the leg of length 5. Name the solid. Give its radius, height and slant height.

8.3 - I can find the surface area of solids.

13. A box is 9 m by 4 m by 3 m. Find its surface area.

14. A triangular prism has a right-triangle base with legs 6 and 8 and hypotenuse 10. The prism is 15 long. Find its surface area.

15. A cylinder has radius 3 ft and height 10 ft. Find its surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

16. A square pyramid has a base side of 10 in and a slant height of 13 in. Find its surface area.

17. A cone has radius 7 cm and slant height 25 cm. Find its surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

18. A sphere has a diameter of 18 in. Find its surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

8.4 - I can find the volume of prisms and cylinders.

19. A cylinder has a volume of 245 π cm3 and a radius of 7 cm. Find its height.

20. A triangular prism has a triangle base with base 10 and height 6. The prism is 9 long. Find its volume.

21. A cylinder has radius 4 m and height 11 m. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

22. A slanted cylinder has radius 5 in, a slant length of 15 in and a vertical height of 12 in. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

23. A concrete slab is 24 ft by 15 ft and 6 in thick. Find its volume in cubic yards (27 ft3 = 1 yd3). Round up to the next whole yard for the order.

24. A pool is 30 ft by 15 ft and the water is 5 ft deep. How many gallons does it hold? Use 1 ft3 ≈ 7.48 gallons.

8.5 - I can find the volume of pyramids, cones, spheres, and composite solids.

25. A square pyramid has a base side of 12 and a height of 9. Find its volume.

26. A cone has radius 9 cm and height 10 cm. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

27. A square pyramid has a base side of 16 and a slant height of 17. Find its height, then its volume.

28. A sphere has radius 6 in. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

29. A hemisphere has radius 9 ft. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

30. A cylinder with radius 3 and height 8 has a cone on top with radius 3 and height 4. Find the volume of the solid. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

8.6 - I can use density, scale, and modeling to solve real problems.

31. A concrete slab is 20 ft by 10 ft and 4 in thick. Concrete weighs 150 lb per cubic foot. Find the weight of the slab to the nearest pound.

32. An object has a mass of 360 g and a volume of 400 cm3. Find its density. Does it float in water?

33. A city has 150,000 people in 60 square miles. Find its population density.

34. A small box has a volume of 30 cm3. A similar box is 4 times as long in every direction. Find the volume of the large box. By what factor does its surface area grow?

35. A model ship is 150 of the size of the real ship. The real ship has a hull area of 5000 ft2 and a volume of 200,000 ft3. Find the model's hull area and volume.

36. Two cans each hold 300 cm3. Can A has radius 3 cm. Can B has radius 5 cm. Which uses less metal? Round to the nearest tenth.
