8.4  Volume of Prisms and Cylinders

Guided Notes - Unit 8: Area, Surface Area, and Volume - DHS Geometry
Teacher Key: answers in red
Objectives
  • Find the volume of a prism with V = Bh
  • Find the volume of a cylinder with V = πr2h
  • Find the volume of a slanted (oblique) prism or cylinder
  • Change cubic units and solve real volume problems

Warm-Up

EX 1. 1. Find the area of a circle with radius 3 (exact).   2. Find the area of a trapezoid with parallel sides 6 and 10 and height 4.   3. Solve x2 + 5x − 14 = 0.

1. 9 π

2. 12(16)(4) = 32

3. (x + 7)(x − 2) = 0, so x = −7 or 2

8.4.1Volume of a Prism

  • Volume is the amount of space inside a solid. We count cubic units.
  • Each layer of the box below has 4 · 3 = 12 cubes. There are 2 layers. Total: 24 cubes.
  • For any prism, V = Bh. B is the area of the base.
EX 2. Find the volume of a box 6 ft long, 4 ft wide, and 3 ft tall.

B = 6 · 4 = 24 square feet

V = Bh = 24 · 3 = 72 cubic feet

EX 3. Find the volume of a box 7 m long, 5 m wide, and 2 m tall.your turn

B = 35. V = 35 · 2 = 70 cubic meters.

EX 4. The base is a right triangle with legs 6 in and 4 in. The prism is 9 in long. Find the volume.

B = 12(6)(4) = 12 square inches

Height of the prism = 9

V = 12 · 9 = 108 cubic inches

EX 5. The base of a prism is a right triangle with legs 5 ft and 12 ft. The prism is 6 ft long. Find the volume.your turn

B = 12(5)(12) = 30. V = 30 · 6 = 180 cubic feet.

EX 6. The base of a prism is the trapezoid with vertices (0, 0), (10, 0), (8, 4), (2, 4). Each unit is 1 ft. The prism is 6 ft tall. Find the volume.

Bottom = 10 − 0 = 10   Top = 8 − 2 = 6   Height = 4

B = 12(10 + 6)(4) = 32 square feet

V = Bh = 32 · 6 = 192 cubic feet

EX 7. The base of a prism is the triangle with vertices (0, 0), (8, 0), (0, 5). Each unit is 1 ft. The prism is 10 ft tall. Find the volume.your turn

B = 12(8)(5) = 20. V = 20 · 10 = 200 cubic feet.

8.4.2Volume of a Cylinder

The base of a cylinder is a circle, so B = πr2 and V = πr2h.
EX 8. Find the volume of a cylinder with r = 3 cm and h = 10 cm. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

V = π(3)2(10) = 90 π

≈ 282.7 cubic centimeters

EX 9. A cylinder has diameter 10 in and height 4 in. Find the volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.your turn

r = 5. V = π(25)(4) = 100 π ≈ 314.2 cubic inches.

8.4.3Slanted Solids

  • Cavalieri's principle: two solids with the same height and the same cross-section area at every level have the same volume.
  • A leaning solid is oblique. Use the straight-up height, not the slanted side.
EX 10. An oblique cylinder has radius 4 ft, a slanted side of 13 ft, and a straight-up height of 12 ft. Find the volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

The 13 ft side is not used.

V = π(4)2(12) = 192 π

≈ 603.2 cubic feet

EX 11. An oblique prism has a rectangular base 6 ft by 5 ft. The slanted edge is 12 ft. The straight-up height is 9 ft. Find the volume.your turn

B = 30. V = 30 · 9 = 270 cubic feet. The 12 ft edge is not used.

8.4.4Changing Units

  • 1 yd3 = 27 ft3 because 3 · 3 · 3 = 27.
  • 1 ft3 = 1728 in3 because 12 · 12 · 12.
  • 1 ft3 ≈ 7.48 gallons.
  • Make all lengths the same unit before you multiply. 4 in = 13 ft.
EX 12. Moses pours a patio 12 ft by 20 ft and 4 in thick. How many cubic yards of concrete? Round to the nearest tenth.

V = 12 · 20 · 13 = 80 cubic feet

80 ÷ 27 ≈ 3.0 cubic yards

EX 13. A patio is 10 ft by 15 ft and 4 in thick. How many cubic yards of concrete? Round to the nearest tenth.your turn

10 · 15 · 13 = 50 cubic feet. 50 ÷ 27 ≈ 1.9 cubic yards.

EX 14. A round pool has diameter 24 ft. The water is 4 ft deep. How many gallons does it hold? Round to the nearest hundred gallons.

r = 12   V = π(12)2(4) = 576 π ≈ 1809.6 cubic feet

1809.6 · 7.48 ≈ 13,535 ≈ 13,500 gallons

EX 15. A round tub has radius 3 ft. The water is 2 ft deep. How many gallons does it hold? Round to the nearest gallon.your turn

V = π(9)(2) = 18 π ≈ 56.5 cubic feet. 56.5 · 7.48 ≈ 423 gallons.

8.4.5Finding a Missing Dimension

Write V = Bh with the numbers you know. Put in the volume. Solve. Change gallons to cubic feet first.
EX 16. A cylinder tank has radius 4 ft and holds 5000 gallons. How tall is it? Round to the nearest tenth.

5000 ÷ 7.48 ≈ 668.4 cubic feet

π(4)2h = 668.4, so h = 668.4 ÷ 16π ≈ 13.3 ft

EX 17. A cylinder tank has radius 3 ft and holds 2000 gallons. How tall is it? Round to the nearest tenth.your turn

2000 ÷ 7.48 ≈ 267.4 cubic feet. h = 267.4 ÷ 9π ≈ 9.5 ft.

EX 18. A box has length x + 3, width x, and height 4. The volume is 160 cubic units. Find x.

4x(x + 3) = 160, so x2 + 3x − 40 = 0

(x + 8)(x − 5) = 0

x = 5 (a length cannot be negative). The box is 8 by 5 by 4.

EX 19. A box has length x + 2, width x, and height 3. The volume is 105 cubic units. Find x.your turn

3x(x + 2) = 105, so x2 + 2x − 35 = 0, so (x + 7)(x − 5) = 0. x = 5.

Summary
basecubicheightobliquestraight-up27

1. V = Bh. B is the area of the base.

2. A cylinder has V = πr2h. Volume is in cubic units.

3. For an oblique solid, use the straight-up height.

4. One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet.

8.4 Volume of Prisms and Cylinders - Practice

Practice

Volume of Prisms

1. A box is 9 cm by 5 cm by 4 cm. Find the volume.

9 · 5 · 4 = 180 cubic centimeters

2. A cube has edge 6.5 m. Find the volume to the nearest tenth.

6.53 = 274.625 ≈ 274.6 cubic meters

3. The base of a prism is a right triangle with legs 7 in and 24 in. The prism is 10 in long. Find the volume.

B = 12(7)(24) = 84. V = 840 cubic inches

4. The base of a prism is a trapezoid with parallel sides 6 ft and 10 ft. The trapezoid is 5 ft tall. The prism is 12 ft long. Find the volume.

B = 12(16)(5) = 40. V = 480 cubic feet

5. The base of a prism is the triangle with vertices (0, 0), (6, 0), (2, 5). Each unit is 1 ft. The prism is 10 ft tall. Find the volume.

Base 6, height 5. B = 15. V = 150 cubic feet

6. The base of a prism is a regular hexagon with side 4 cm. The prism is 10 cm tall. Give the exact volume and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

a = 2√3, P = 24. B = 12(2√3)(24) = 24√3. V = 240√3 ≈ 415.7 cubic centimeters

7. A box has a base 9 ft by 5 ft and a volume of 180 cubic feet. Find its height.

45h = 180, so h = 4 ft

8. A box has length x + 1, width x, and height 6. The volume is 336 cubic units. Find x.

6x(x + 1) = 336, x2 + x − 56 = 0, (x + 8)(x − 7) = 0, x = 7

Volume of Cylinders

Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

9. r = 6 ft and h = 5 ft.

180 π ≈ 565.5 cubic feet

10. The diameter is 14 cm and the height is 20 cm.

r = 7. π(49)(20) = 980 π ≈ 3078.8 cubic centimeters

11. A cylinder with radius 5 in has volume 250 π cubic inches. Find its height.

25πh = 250π, so h = 10 in

12. A cylinder with height 6 m has volume 96 π cubic meters. Find its radius.

6πr2 = 96π, r2 = 16, r = 4 m

13. Find the mistake. Esperanza finds the volume of a cylinder with diameter 10 cm and height 4 cm. She writes V = π(10)2(4) = 400 π. What is the correct volume?

She used the diameter as the radius. r = 5, so V = π(25)(4) = 100 π ≈ 314.2 cubic centimeters.

14. Six cans fit in a box, as in the top view. Each can has diameter 4 in and height 6 in. The box is 12 in by 8 in by 6 in. What percent of the box do the cans fill? Round to the nearest tenth of a percent.

Cans: 6 · π(4)(6) = 144 π ≈ 452.4. Box: 12 · 8 · 6 = 576. 452.4 ÷ 576 ≈ 78.5%

Slanted Solids

15. An oblique cylinder has radius 3 in, a slanted side of 10 in, and a straight-up height of 8 in. Find the volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

π(9)(8) = 72 π ≈ 226.2 cubic inches

16. An oblique prism has a rectangular base 6 m by 5 m. The slanted edge is 12 m. The straight-up height is 9 m. Find the volume.

30 · 9 = 270 cubic meters

Real Problems

17. A slab is 15 ft by 10 ft and 6 in thick. How many cubic yards of concrete? Round to the nearest tenth.

15 · 10 · 0.5 = 75 cubic feet. 75 ÷ 27 ≈ 2.8 cubic yards

18. A driveway is 24 ft by 10 ft and 5 in thick. How many cubic yards of concrete? Round to the nearest tenth.

24 · 10 · 512 = 100 cubic feet. 100 ÷ 27 ≈ 3.7 cubic yards

19. A raised garden bed is 8 ft by 4 ft and filled 1.5 ft deep with soil. A bag of soil holds 2 cubic feet. How many bags are needed?

8 · 4 · 1.5 = 48 cubic feet. 48 ÷ 2 = 24 bags

20. An aquarium is 48 in long, 18 in wide, and 24 in tall. How many gallons does it hold when it is full? Use 1 ft3 ≈ 7.48 gallons. Round to the nearest gallon.

48 · 18 · 24 = 20,736 in3. 20,736 ÷ 1728 = 12 ft3. 12 · 7.48 = 89.76 ≈ 90 gallons

21. A round pool has diameter 15 ft and water 3.5 ft deep. How many gallons does it hold? Use 1 ft3 ≈ 7.48 gallons. Round to the nearest hundred.

r = 7.5. V = π(56.25)(3.5) ≈ 618.5 ft3. 618.5 · 7.48 ≈ 4626, so about 4,600 gallons

22. This is the side view of a pool. The pool is 15 ft wide. Find the area of the side view, then the volume, then the gallons. Round the gallons to the nearest hundred.

Side view is a trapezoid: 12(3 + 8)(30) = 165 ft2. V = 165 · 15 = 2475 ft3. 2475 · 7.48 = 18,513, so about 18,500 gallons

23. A cylinder tank has radius 5 ft and holds 4000 gallons. How tall is it? Round to the nearest tenth.

4000 ÷ 7.48 ≈ 534.8 ft3. h = 534.8 ÷ 25π ≈ 6.8 ft

Challenge

26. A cylinder has radius r and height h. How many times larger is the volume when the radius is doubled and the height stays the same? When both are doubled?

π(2r)2h = 4πr2h: 4 times. Both doubled: π(2r)2(2h) = 8πr2h: 8 times.

27. A metal pipe is a cylinder with a smaller cylinder cut out of it. The outer radius is 3 cm, the inner radius is 2 cm, and the pipe is 10 cm long. Find the volume of metal. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

π(9)(10) − π(4)(10) = 90 π − 40 π = 50 π ≈ 157.1 cubic centimeters
