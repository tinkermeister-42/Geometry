8.3.1Nets

  • A net is a solid cut open and laid flat. It shows every face.
  • The surface area of a solid is the area of its net.
EX 2. Which nets fold into a cube?

A: yes. 4 squares make a band, 1 top, 1 bottom.

B: no. Both extra squares are on top, so the bottom is empty.

C: yes. 1 extra square is the top, 1 is the bottom.

EX 3. A net has two triangles and three rectangles. Which solid does it fold into?your turn

A triangular prism.

8.3.2Prisms

  • Lateral area L = Ph, where P is the perimeter of the base.
  • Surface area SA = Ph + 2B, where B is the area of one base.
EX 4. Find the surface area of this box.

Top and bottom: 2(8 · 5) = 80

Front and back: 2(8 · 3) = 48

Left and right: 2(5 · 3) = 30

SA = 158 square inches

EX 5. Find the surface area of a box that is 9 in by 6 in by 4 in.your turn

2(54) + 2(36) + 2(24) = 108 + 72 + 48 = 228 square inches

EX 6. The bases are right triangles with sides 3, 4, and 5 cm. The prism is 10 cm long. Find the surface area.

B = 12(4)(3) = 6

P = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12

L = Ph = 12 · 10 = 120

SA = 120 + 2(6) = 132 square centimeters

EX 7. The bases are right triangles with sides 5, 12, and 13 cm. The prism is 8 cm long. Find the surface area.your turn

B = 12(12)(5) = 30. P = 30. L = 30 · 8 = 240.

SA = 240 + 2(30) = 300 square centimeters

8.3.3Cylinders

The label of a can unrolls into a rectangle. Its width is 2πr and its height is h.
  • Lateral area = 2πrh
  • SA = 2πr2 + 2πrh
EX 8. Find the surface area of a cylinder with r = 3 ft and h = 8 ft. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

SA = 2π(3)2 + 2π(3)(8)

= 18 π + 48 π = 66 π

≈ 207.3 square feet

EX 9. Find the surface area of a cylinder with r = 4 in and h = 11 in. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.your turn

2π(16) + 2π(4)(11) = 32 π + 88 π = 120 π ≈ 377.0 square inches

EX 10. A closed water tank has radius 10 ft and height 12 ft. One gallon of paint covers 350 square feet. How many whole gallons does Samir need for the whole outside?

SA = 2π(10)2 + 2π(10)(12) = 440 π

≈ 1382.3 square feet

1382.3 ÷ 350 ≈ 3.95

Gallons to buy: 4 (round up)

EX 11. A can has radius 4 cm and height 12 cm. The paper label covers only the side. Find the area of the label. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.your turn

2π(4)(12) = 96 π ≈ 301.6 square centimeters

8.3.4Pyramids, Cones, and Spheres

  • The slant height ℓ is measured along a slanted face. Find it with the Pythagorean theorem.
  • Regular pyramid: SA = B + 12Pℓ
  • Cone: SA = πr2 + πrℓ
  • Sphere: SA = 4πr2
EX 12. A square pyramid has base side 6 ft and slant height 5 ft. Find the surface area.

B = 36   P = 24

SA = 36 + 12(24)(5) = 96 square feet

EX 13. A square pyramid has base side 10 ft and slant height 8 ft. Find the surface area.your turn

100 + 12(40)(8) = 100 + 160 = 260 square feet

EX 14. A square pyramid has base side 10 in and height 12 in. Find the surface area.

Half the base = 5

ℓ2 = 52 + 122 = 169, so ℓ = 13

SA = 100 + 12(40)(13) = 360 square inches

EX 15. A square pyramid has base side 12 in and height 8 in. Find the surface area.your turn

Half the base = 6. ℓ2 = 36 + 64 = 100, so ℓ = 10.

SA = 144 + 12(48)(10) = 384 square inches

EX 16. A cone has radius 4 in and height 3 in. Find the surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

ℓ2 = 42 + 32, so ℓ = 5 in

SA = π(4)2 + π(4)(5) = 16 π + 20 π = 36 π

≈ 113.1 square inches

EX 17. A cone has r = 9 cm and h = 12 cm. Find the surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.your turn

ℓ2 = 81 + 144 = 225, so ℓ = 15.

SA = 81 π + 135 π = 216 π ≈ 678.6 square centimeters

EX 18. Find the mistake. Kofi finds the surface area of the cone in EX 16. He writes SA = π(4)2 + π(4)(3) = 28 π.

He used the height 3. The formula needs the slant height ℓ = 5. The correct answer is 36 π.

EX 19. A basketball has radius 12 cm. Find its surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

4π(12)2 = 576 π ≈ 1809.6 square centimeters

EX 20. A soccer ball has radius 11 cm. Find its surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.your turn

4π(11)2 = 484 π ≈ 1520.5 square centimeters

Why 4πr2?
Peel an orange and press the peel flat. It covers about 4 circles with the same radius as the orange.
Summary
basenetperimeterslant heightsquare

1. A net is a solid laid flat. Surface area is the area of the net.

2. Prism: SA = Ph + 2B. P is the perimeter of the base.

3. Pyramids and cones use the slant height, not the height.

4. Surface area is measured in square units.

8.3 Surface Area and Nets - Practice

Practice

Nets

1. Name the solid that each net folds into. a: triangular pyramid. b: cone. c: rectangular prism.

2. Which of the nets D, E, and F fold into a cube? Explain what is wrong with the other one.

D and E fold into a cube. F is two rows of 3. It has no way to make a band of 4 squares, so faces overlap.

3. A net is two circles and one rectangle. The circles have radius 3 in. How long is the rectangle? Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

2π(3) = 6 π ≈ 18.8 inches (the circumference)

Prisms

4. Find the surface area of a box that is 10 cm by 6 cm by 4 cm.

2(60) + 2(40) + 2(24) = 248 square centimeters

5. Find the surface area of a cube with edge 7.5 in.

6(7.5)2 = 337.5 square inches

6. The bases of a prism are right triangles with sides 6, 8, and 10 ft. The prism is 15 ft long. Find the surface area.

B = 24, P = 24, L = 360. SA = 360 + 48 = 408 square feet

7. A rectangular prism has a base 12 in by 5 in and a height of 9 in. Find the lateral area.

P = 34. L = 34 · 9 = 306 square inches

8. Lucía wraps a gift box that is 14 in by 10 in by 6 in. She buys 10% more paper than the surface area, to allow for overlap. How much paper does she buy? Round to the nearest square inch.

SA = 2(140) + 2(84) + 2(60) = 568. 568 · 1.10 = 624.8, about 625 square inches

9. A box has a base 6 ft by 4 ft and a surface area of 148 square feet. Find its height.

2(24) + 2(6h) + 2(4h) = 148, so 48 + 20h = 148, so h = 5 ft

10. Find the mistake. Moses says the surface area of a box 5 ft by 3 ft by 2 ft is 5 · 3 · 2 = 30 square feet. What did he find, and what is the correct surface area?

He multiplied the three lengths. That is not an area. SA = 2(15) + 2(10) + 2(6) = 62 square feet.

Cylinders

11. Find the surface area of a cylinder with r = 5 in and h = 9 in. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

50 π + 90 π = 140 π ≈ 439.8 square inches

12. A soup can has radius 3.4 cm and height 10 cm. Find the area of its label (the side only). Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

2π(3.4)(10) = 68 π ≈ 213.6 square centimeters

13. A closed tank has radius 6 ft and height 15 ft. One gallon of paint covers 300 square feet. How many whole gallons are needed to paint the whole outside?

SA = 72 π + 180 π = 252 π ≈ 791.7. 791.7 ÷ 300 ≈ 2.64, so 3 gallons

14. A cylinder with radius 4 m has a surface area of 96 π square meters. Find its height.

32 π + 8 πh = 96 π, so 8 πh = 64 π, so h = 8 m

Pyramids, Cones, and Spheres

15. A square pyramid has base side 8 in and slant height 7 in. Find the surface area.

64 + 12(32)(7) = 64 + 112 = 176 square inches

16. A square pyramid has base side 16 cm and height 15 cm. Find the slant height and the surface area.

ℓ2 = 64 + 225 = 289, ℓ = 17. SA = 256 + 12(64)(17) = 800 square centimeters

17. A cone has radius 3 ft and slant height 7 ft. Find the surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

9 π + 21 π = 30 π ≈ 94.2 square feet

18. A cone has radius 8 cm and height 15 cm. Find the surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

ℓ = 17. SA = 64 π + 136 π = 200 π ≈ 628.3 square centimeters

19. The triangle with vertices (0, 0), (6, 0), and (0, 8) spins around the y-axis. Find the surface area of the cone. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

r = 6, h = 8, ℓ = 10. SA = 36 π + 60 π = 96 π ≈ 301.6 square units

20. Find the mistake. Ana says a cone with r = 5 and h = 12 has SA = π(5)2 + π(5)(12) = 85 π. Find the correct surface area.

She used the height. ℓ = 13, so SA = 25 π + 65 π = 90 π ≈ 282.7 square units.

21. A tent is a cone with no floor. The radius is 7 ft and the height is 24 ft. How much fabric does it use? Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

ℓ = 25. π(7)(25) = 175 π ≈ 549.8 square feet

22. A sphere has radius 9 in. Find its surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

4π(81) = 324 π ≈ 1017.9 square inches

23. A sphere has surface area 100 π square centimeters. Find its radius.

4πr2 = 100 π, r2 = 25, r = 5 cm

24. A dome is half a sphere with radius 12 ft. It has no floor. Find the area of the curved surface. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

12(4π)(144) = 288 π ≈ 904.8 square feet

Challenge

27. A box is 2 by 3 by 4. Find its surface area. Then double every length and find the new surface area. How many times larger is it?

2(6) + 2(8) + 2(12) = 52. Doubled box 4 by 6 by 8: 2(24) + 2(32) + 2(48) = 208. 208 ÷ 52 = 4 times larger.

28. The side of a cone unrolls into part of a circle with radius ℓ and arc length 2πr. A cone has r = 3 and ℓ = 9. What fraction of a full circle with radius 9 is the unrolled side? What is the angle of that piece?

Arc 2π(3) = 6 π. Full circle 2π(9) = 18 π. Fraction 618 = 13. Angle 360° ÷ 3 = 120°.
