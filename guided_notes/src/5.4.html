  • If the ratios are not equal, the segment is {{a}}not{{/a}} parallel to the side.

5.4.3The Midsegment of a Triangle

  • A midsegment joins the {{a}}midpoints{{/a}} of two sides of a triangle.
  • The midsegment is {{a}}parallel{{/a}} to the third side.
  • The midsegment is {{a}}half{{/a}} as long as the third side.
{{fig 5.4_midseg|triangle ABC with D the midpoint of AB and E the midpoint of AC; DE is parallel to BC|2.6in}}
EX 5. Use the midsegment. D and E are the midpoints of {{seg AB}} and {{seg AC}}.
a. BC = 18. Find DE.
b. DE = 3x + 1 and BC = 5x + 12. Find x and BC.
{{a}}

b. BC = 2 · DE, so 5x + 12 = 2(3x + 1).

5x + 12 = 6x + 2, so x = 10.

BC = 5(10) + 12 = 62. (Check: DE = 31.)

{{/a}}

{{/a}}
