7.3.1What Is a Radian?

  • Lay the {{a}}radius{{/a}} along the edge of a circle. The angle at the center is 1 radian.
  • Radian measure: θ = {{f {{a}}s{{/a}}/{{a}}r{{/a}}}}, where s is the arc length.
  • The circumference is 2 πr, so a full turn is {{a}}2 π{{/a}} radians ≈ {{a}}6.28{{/a}}.
