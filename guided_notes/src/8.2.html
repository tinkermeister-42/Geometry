8.2.1Solids

  • A solid is a {{a}}three-dimensional{{/a}} shape.
  • A polyhedron has only {{a}}flat{{/a}} faces. Prisms and {{a}}pyramids{{/a}} are polyhedrons.
  • A face is a flat surface. An edge is where two {{a}}faces{{/a}} meet. A vertex is a {{a}}corner{{/a}}.
  • A prism has {{a}}two{{/a}} parallel bases that are the same shape. A pyramid has {{a}}one{{/a}} base and triangle faces that meet at a point.

Summary
axiscirclecross sectionedgesspheretriangle

1. The flat shape made by slicing a solid is a {{a}}cross section{{/a}}.

2. Every slice of a sphere is a {{a}}circle{{/a}}. A slice of a cone through its apex is a {{a}}triangle{{/a}}.

3. Euler: F + V − E = 2, where E counts {{a}}edges{{/a}}.

4. A shape spins around an {{a}}axis{{/a}}. A semicircle makes a {{a}}sphere{{/a}}.
