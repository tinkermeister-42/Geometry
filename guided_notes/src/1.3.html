1.3.1Complementary and Supplementary Angles

  • Two angles are complementary if their measures add to {{a}}90°{{/a}}.
  • Two angles are supplementary if their measures add to {{a}}180°{{/a}}.
  • The angles {{a}}do not have to touch{{/a}}.
  • Complement of x°: {{a}}(90 − x)°{{/a}}   Supplement of x°: {{a}}(180 − x)°{{/a}}
{{fig 1.3_comp_supp|a 25 degree angle and a 65 degree angle, complementary; a 110 degree angle and a 70 degree angle, supplementary|6in}}

1.3.2Adjacent Angles and Linear Pairs

  • Adjacent angles share a {{a}}vertex{{/a}} and a {{a}}side{{/a}}. They do not {{a}}overlap{{/a}}.
  • A linear pair is two adjacent angles whose outside sides are {{a}}opposite rays{{/a}}.
  • The angles in a linear pair are {{a}}supplementary{{/a}}: they add to {{a}}180°{{/a}}.

1.3.3Vertical Angles

  • Vertical angles are the angles {{a}}across from each other{{/a}} when two lines cross.
  • Vertical angles are {{a}}equal{{/a}}.
  • Name the vertical pairs: ∠1 and {{a}}∠3{{/a}}, ∠2 and {{a}}∠4{{/a}}.
{{fig 1.3_vertical|two crossing lines making four angles numbered 1 to 4|2.3in}}
EX 6. {{mang 1}} = 60°. Find {{mang 2}}, {{mang 3}}, and {{mang 4}}. Use the figure above.

∠1 and ∠2 are a {{a}}linear pair{{/a}}: {{mang 2}} = {{a}}120°{{/a}}

∠3 is {{a}}vertical{{/a}} to ∠1: {{mang 3}} = {{a}}60°{{/a}}

∠4 is vertical to ∠2: {{mang 4}} = {{a}}120°{{/a}}

EX 7. Suppose {{mang 2}} = 135°. Find {{mang 1}}, {{mang 3}}, and {{mang 4}}.your turn
{{a}}

{{mang 1}} = 180 − 135 = 45°.  {{mang 3}} = 45°.  {{mang 4}} = 135°.

{{/a}}
EX 8. Why are vertical angles equal? Call the right angle x.
{{fig 1.3_vertical_why|two crossing lines; the right and left angles are x, the top and bottom angles are 180 minus x|2.8in}}

The top angle is a linear pair with x: {{a}}180 − x{{/a}}

The left angle is a linear pair with the top angle: 180 − (180 − x) = {{a}}x{{/a}}

So the left and right angles are {{a}}equal{{/a}}.

Summary
90°180°equallinear pairsupplementaryvertical

1. Complementary angles add to {{a}}90°{{/a}}. Supplementary angles add to {{a}}180°{{/a}}.

2. A {{a}}linear pair{{/a}} makes a straight line, so its angles are {{a}}supplementary{{/a}}.

3. {{a}}Vertical{{/a}} angles are across from each other and are {{a}}equal{{/a}}.

4. To solve for x: name the pair, pick the rule, write the equation.

Real Problems

23. Mohammed builds a sawhorse. The legs cross in an X. One angle between the legs is 68°. Find the other three angles.

{{a}}

The angle across is 68°. The two angles next to it are 180 − 68 = 112° each.

{{/a}}

24. A pair of scissors has handles that make an angle of 38°. The blades make an angle vertical to it. Find the angle between the blades. Then find the angle next to it.

{{a}}

The blades make 38°. The angle next to it is 180 − 38 = 142°.

Find the Mistake

25. Samir has a linear pair, (2x + 10)° and (3x − 20)°. He writes 2x + 10 = 3x − 20 and gets x = 30. What did he do wrong? Find the correct x.

{{a}}

He treated a linear pair as equal. A linear pair adds to 180: 5x − 10 = 180, so x = 38. The angles are 86° and 94°.

{{/a}}
