Summary
adjacentcosinedividehypotenusemultiplyoppositesinetangent

1. To choose a ratio, name the two sides: the one you know and the one you want. Opposite and hypotenuse mean {{a}}sine{{/a}}.

2. Adjacent and hypotenuse mean {{a}}cosine{{/a}}. Opposite and adjacent mean {{a}}tangent{{/a}}.

3. If the unknown is on top, {{a}}multiply{{/a}}. If the unknown is on the bottom, {{a}}divide{{/a}}.

4. The {{a}}hypotenuse{{/a}} is always the longest side, so use it to check an answer.
