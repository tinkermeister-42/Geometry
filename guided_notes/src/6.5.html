6.5.1The Acute Angles Are Complementary

  • The angles of a triangle add to {{a}}180°{{/a}}. A right triangle has a {{a}}90°{{/a}} angle.
  • So the two acute angles add to {{a}}90°{{/a}}. They are {{a}}complementary{{/a}}.
  • m∠B = 90° − {{a}}m∠A{{/a}}

Summary
45°complementcomplementarycosinereciprocalssine

1. The acute angles of a right triangle are {{a}}complementary{{/a}}. They add to 90°.

2. sin A = cos(90° − A). The sine of an angle is the {{a}}cosine{{/a}} of its {{a}}complement{{/a}}.

3. If sin(one angle) = cos(another), the two angles add to 90°.

4. tan A = {{f sin A/cos A}}, and tan A and tan(90° − A) are {{a}}reciprocals{{/a}}.
