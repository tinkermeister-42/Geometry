Summary

1. A pyramid or cone holds {{a}}one third{{/a}} of the prism or cylinder with the same base and height.

2. For a sphere, the radius is {{a}}cubed{{/a}}: V = {{f 4/3}}πr3.

3. Find the height from the slant height with the {{a}}Pythagorean theorem{{/a}}.

4. For composite solids, {{a}}add{{/a}} pieces or {{a}}subtract{{/a}} a removed piece.
