6.2.1Naming the Sides

  • The {{a}}hypotenuse{{/a}} is across from the right angle. It is the longest side.
  • The {{a}}opposite{{/a}} side is across from the angle you choose.
  • The {{a}}adjacent{{/a}} side is the leg that touches the angle you choose.
  • When you pick the other acute angle, the opposite and adjacent sides {{a}}trade places{{/a}}.

Summary
adjacentanglefractionhypotenuseoppositeslopetangent

1. In a right triangle, the longest side is the {{a}}hypotenuse{{/a}}.

2. Sine is {{a}}opposite{{/a}} over hypotenuse. Cosine is {{a}}adjacent{{/a}} over hypotenuse. Tangent is opposite over adjacent.

3. A ratio depends only on the {{a}}angle{{/a}}, because right triangles with the same angle are similar.

4. The {{a}}tangent{{/a}} of an angle is the {{a}}slope{{/a}} of the line.
