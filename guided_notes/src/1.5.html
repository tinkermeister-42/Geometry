1.5.3Exterior Angles

An exterior angle is outside the triangle, next to an inside angle. The two inside angles far from it are the {{a}}remote interior{{/a}} angles.

Exterior angle = {{a}}the sum of the two remote angles{{/a}}

Summary
180°360°baseexteriorremoteregular

1. The angles of a triangle add to {{a}}180°{{/a}}.

2. The {{a}}base{{/a}} angles of an isosceles triangle are equal.

3. An {{a}}exterior{{/a}} angle equals the sum of the two {{a}}remote{{/a}} interior angles.

4. The angles of an n-sided polygon add to (n − 2) · 180°. The exterior angles add to {{a}}360°{{/a}}.

5. All angles of a {{a}}regular{{/a}} polygon are equal.

Challenge

29. Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 5 times its exterior angle. Find the number of sides.
{{a}}

Interior + exterior = 180, so 6 × exterior = 180 and exterior = 30°. n = 360 ÷ 30 = 12.

{{/a}}
30. Use the interior angle sum to explain why the exterior angles of a polygon add to 360°. (Hint: at each corner, interior plus exterior is 180°.)
{{a}}

n corners give n · 180. Take away the interior sum (n − 2) · 180. What is left is 2 · 180 = 360°.

{{/a}}
