2.8.1Perimeter from Vertices

  • Perimeter is the {{a}}sum{{/a}} of the side lengths.
  • Use the distance formula for each {{a}}slanted{{/a}} side.
  • Give an exact answer, then round if the problem asks.
EX 1. Find the perimeter of triangle ABC with A(−2, 1), B(4, 1), and C(4, 5). Give the exact answer and round to the nearest tenth.

AB = |4 − (−2)| = {{a}}6{{/a}}. BC = |5 − 1| = {{a}}4{{/a}}.

AC = 62 + 42 = {{a}}52{{/a}} = {{a}}213{{/a}}.

Perimeter = 6 + 4 + 213 = {{a}}10 + 213{{/a}} ≈ {{a}}17.2{{/a}}.

2.8.2Area with Horizontal and Vertical Sides

  • Rectangle: A = bh. Triangle: A = 12bh.
  • Trapezoid: A = 12(b1 + b2)h, where b1 and b2 are the {{a}}parallel{{/a}} sides.
  • The height is the {{a}}perpendicular{{/a}} distance between the bases.
EX 4. Find the area of the trapezoid with vertices A(1, 1), B(9, 1), C(7, 5), and D(3, 5).
{{fig 2.8_trap|trapezoid ABCD with A(1,1), B(9,1), C(7,5), D(3,5)|2.4in}}

AB and DC are horizontal, so they are parallel. b1 = 9 − 1 = 8 and b2 = 7 − 3 = {{a}}4{{/a}}. The height is 5 − 1 = {{a}}4{{/a}}.

A = 12(8 + 4)(4) = {{a}}24{{/a}} square units.

EX 5. Find the area of the right triangle with vertices (1, 1), (6, 1), and (6, 4).

Base: 6 − 1 = 5. Height: 4 − 1 = 3.

A = 12(5)(3) = {{a}}7.5{{/a}} square units.

EX 6. Find the area of the triangle with vertices (−2, −1), (4, −1), and (4, 3).your turn
{{a}}

Base = 6, height = 4. A = 12(6)(4) = 12 square units.

2.8.3Slanted Figures: The Box Method

  • Step 1: draw the {{a}}bounding box{{/a}} and find its area.
  • Step 2: find the area of each {{a}}corner{{/a}} triangle outside the figure.
  • Step 3: {{a}}subtract{{/a}}.
EX 7. Find the area of the triangle with vertices A(1, 1), B(7, 3), and C(3, 6).
{{fig 2.8_box|triangle ABC with A(1,1), B(7,3), C(3,6) inside a box|2.6in}}

Bounding box: x from 1 to 7 and y from 1 to 6. Area = 6 · 5 = {{a}}30{{/a}}.

Corner triangles: bottom right, legs 6 and 2: 12(6)(2) = {{a}}6{{/a}}. Top right, legs 4 and 3: 12(4)(3) = {{a}}6{{/a}}. Top left, legs 2 and 5: 12(2)(5) = {{a}}5{{/a}}.

Total outside: 6 + 6 + 5 = 17. Area of △ ABC = 30 − 17 = {{a}}13{{/a}} square units.

EX 8. Find the area of the triangle with vertices (0, 0), (7, 1), and (3, 6) by the box method.your turn
{{a}}

Box: 7 · 6 = 42. Corners: 12(7)(1) = 3.5, 12(4)(5) = 10, 12(3)(6) = 9. Total 22.5. Area = 42 − 22.5 = 19.5 square units.

{{/a}}

2.8.4Slanted Squares and Rectangles

  • Area of a rectangle = {{a}}length{{/a}} × {{a}}width{{/a}}. Find both with the distance formula.
  • Check with the {{a}}box{{/a}} method. Both answers should match.
EX 9. Find the area of the rectangle with vertices A(1, 3), B(3, 1), C(7, 5), and D(5, 7). Use two methods.
{{fig 2.8_slanted|rectangle ABCD with A(1,3), B(3,1), C(7,5), D(5,7) inside a 6 by 6 box|2.6in}}

Method 1, side lengths. AB = 22 + 22 = 8 = 22. BC = 42 + 42 = 32 = {{a}}42{{/a}}.

A = 22 · 42 = 8 · 2 = {{a}}16{{/a}} square units.

Method 2, box. The box has x from 1 to 7 and y from 1 to 7. It is 6 · 6 = 36. The four corner triangles have areas 2, 8, 2, 8. Their total is 20. 36 − 20 = {{a}}16{{/a}}.

Both methods give 16.

EX 10. A garden on a property map has corners A(0, 0), B(10, 0), C(10, 6), and D(0, 3). Each unit is 1 foot. How much fence goes around it? How many square feet of mulch cover it? Round to the nearest tenth.

Sides: AB = 10, BC = 6, DA = 3, and CD = 102 + 32 = 109 ≈ 10.4.

Fence: 10 + 6 + 3 + 10.4 = {{a}}29.4{{/a}} feet.

The garden is a trapezoid with parallel sides DA and BC (both vertical) and height 10. Area = 12(3 + 6)(10) = {{a}}45{{/a}} square feet of mulch.

EX 11. A square has vertices (2, 0), (5, 2), (3, 5), and (0, 3). Find its area with the side length. Then check it with the box method.your turn
{{a}}

Side = 32 + 22 = 13, so area = 13. Box: 5 · 5 = 25. Four triangles with legs 3 and 2 have area 3 each, total 12. 25 − 12 = 13.

{{/a}}
