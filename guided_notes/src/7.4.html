7.4.3Inscribed Quadrilaterals

  • A polygon is inscribed if all of its vertices are {{a}}on the circle{{/a}}.
  • Opposite angles of an inscribed quadrilateral are {{a}}supplementary{{/a}}. They add to {{a}}180°{{/a}}.
  • m∠A + m∠C = {{a}}180°{{/a}} and m∠B + m∠D = {{a}}180°{{/a}}

7.4.4Tangent Lines

  • A line is tangent to a circle if it touches the circle at exactly {{a}}one{{/a}} point. That point is the point of tangency.
  • A tangent is {{a}}perpendicular{{/a}} to the radius at the point of tangency.
  • Two tangent segments from one outside point are {{a}}equal{{/a}}.

1. An inscribed angle is {{a}}half{{/a}} of its intercepted arc.

2. An angle in a semicircle measures {{a}}90°{{/a}}.
