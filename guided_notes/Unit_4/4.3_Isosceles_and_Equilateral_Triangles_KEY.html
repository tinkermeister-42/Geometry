4.3.1Parts of an Isosceles Triangle

  • An isosceles triangle has at least two congruent sides. They are the legs. The third side is the base.
  • The angle between the legs is the vertex angle.
  • The two angles that touch the base are the base angles.
EX 1. Name the parts

AB ≅ AC in the figure. Name the legs, the base, the vertex angle, and the base angles.

Legs: AB and AC

Base: BC

Vertex angle: ∠A

Base angles: ∠B and ∠C

EX 2. In △ PQR, PQ ≅ QR. Name the legs, the base, the vertex angle, and the base angles.your turn

Legs: PQ and QR. Base: PR. Vertex angle: ∠Q. Base angles: ∠P and ∠R.

4.3.2The Base Angles Theorem

  • Base angles theorem: In an isosceles triangle, the base angles are congruent.
  • Converse: If two angles of a triangle are congruent, the sides across from them are congruent.
  • Why it works: fold the triangle along the line from the vertex to the midpoint of the base. This is a reflection (Unit 3). The two halves match, so ∠B lands on ∠C.
  • The vertex angle and the two base angles add to 180°.
EX 3. Find the base angles

The vertex angle of an isosceles triangle is 40°. Find each base angle.

The base angles are equal. Together they measure 180 − 40 = 140°.

Each base angle is 140 ÷ 2 = 70°.

EX 4. Find the vertex angle

A base angle of an isosceles triangle is 52°. Find the vertex angle.

Both base angles are 52°. Their sum is 2(52) = 104°.

The vertex angle is 180 − 104 = 76°.

EX 5. Use the converse

In △ ABC, m∠B = m∠C = 65° and AB = 9. Find AC.

AC is across from ∠B and AB is across from ∠C. The angles are equal, so the sides across from them are equal.

AC = 9

EX 6. Try ityour turn

a. The vertex angle of an isosceles triangle is 80°. Find each base angle.

(180 − 80) ÷ 2 = 50°

b. A base angle of an isosceles triangle is 35°. Find the vertex angle.

180 − 2(35) = 110°

4.3.3Equilateral Triangles

  • An equilateral triangle has three congruent sides. It is also equiangular: all three angles are congruent.
  • Each angle measures 180 ÷ 3 = 60°.
  • If all three angles of a triangle are 60°, the triangle is equilateral.
EX 7. Equilateral with algebra

△ ABC is equilateral. AB = 2x + 1 and BC = 3x − 4. Find x and the perimeter.

All sides are equal, so 2x + 1 = 3x − 4.

5 = x, so x = 5.

Each side is 2(5) + 1 = 11. The perimeter is 3 · 11 = 33.

EX 8. Each angle of an equilateral triangle is (5x − 5)°. Find x.your turn

5x − 5 = 60, so 5x = 65 and x = 13.

A yield sign
A yield sign is an equilateral triangle. If one side is 36 inches, all three sides are 36 inches, and every angle is 60°.

4.3.4Solve for x in Isosceles Triangles

  • Equal sides: set the two sides equal. Equal angles: set the two angles equal.
  • Use the angle sum, 180°, when the vertex angle is also needed.
  • With two triangles that share a side, find the angles in one triangle at a time.
EX 9. Angles with x

AB ≅ AC. The vertex angle is 50°. The base angle ∠B is (3x + 5)°. Find x.

Base angles together: 180 − 50 = 130. Each base angle is 130 ÷ 2 = 65.

3x + 5 = 65, so 3x = 60 and x = 20.

EX 10. Sides with x

AB ≅ AC. AB = 2x + 1, AC = x + 8, and BC = 12. Find x and the perimeter.

The legs are equal, so 2x + 1 = x + 8 and x = 7.

Each leg is 7 + 8 = 15. The perimeter is 15 + 15 + 12 = 42.

EX 11. Two triangles that share a side

AB ≅ BC and AD ≅ DC. m∠B = 40° and m∠D = 100°. Find m∠BAD.

In △ ABC, each base angle is (180 − 40) ÷ 2 = 70°. So m∠BAC = 70°.

In △ ADC, each base angle is (180 − 100) ÷ 2 = 40°. So m∠CAD = 40°.

m∠BAD = 70 + 40 = 110° (angle addition).

EX 12. △ ABC is isosceles with AB = AC. m∠B = (4x − 10)° and m∠C = (2x + 20)°. Find x and the vertex angle.your turn

4x − 10 = 2x + 20, so x = 15. Each base angle is 50°. The vertex angle is 180 − 100 = 80°.

4.3.5Check on a Grid

  • In an isosceles triangle, the line from the vertex angle to the midpoint of the base is perpendicular to the base.
  • To check: find the midpoint of the base. Then find the slopes. Slopes of perpendicular lines multiply to −1.
EX 13. Show it with slopes

A(7, 2), B(3, 5), C(7, 7). Show that △ ABC is isosceles with base BC. Show that the line from A to the midpoint M of BC is perpendicular to BC.

AB = 42 + 32 = 5 and AC = 02 + 52 = 5. The legs are equal.

Midpoint of BC: M = (5, 6)

Slope of BC: 7 − 57 − 3 = 12

Slope of AM: 6 − 25 − 7 = −2

12 · (−2) = −1, so AM ⊥ BC.

EX 14. B(0, 0), C(6, 2), A(4, −2). Show that AB = AC and that the line from A to the midpoint of BC is perpendicular to BC.your turn

AB = 16 + 4 = 20 and AC = 4 + 16 = 20. M = (3, 1). Slope of AM is 1 − (−2)3 − 4 = −3. Slope of BC is 13. The product is −1.

Summary
base anglescongruentequilateralperpendicularreflectionvertex angle

1. The two congruent sides of an isosceles triangle are the legs. The angle between them is the vertex angle. The angles on the base are the base angles.

2. Base angles are congruent. The converse is also true: equal angles mean equal sides. A fold line is a reflection.

3. An equilateral triangle has three 60° angles.

4. The line from the vertex to the midpoint of the base is perpendicular to the base.

11. △ ABC is equilateral. AB = 4x − 3 and BC = 2x + 7. Find x and the perimeter.

13. Ahmad says a triangle with sides 5, 5, and 8 is equilateral. Find his mistake.

An equilateral triangle needs all three sides equal. This triangle is isosceles only, because 8 is different.

Solve for the variable.

14. AB ≅ AC. AB = 3x − 1 and AC = x + 9. Find x and AB.

3x − 1 = x + 9, so x = 5. AB = 14.

15. AB ≅ AC. m∠B = (4x − 10)° and m∠C = 50°. Find x and the vertex angle.

16. The vertex angle of an isosceles triangle is (4x)°. Each base angle is (3x + 10)°. Find x and the three angles.

4x + 2(3x + 10) = 180, so 10x + 20 = 180 and x = 16. The angles are 64°, 58°, and 58°.

17. An isosceles triangle has legs (x + 6) and a base (2x − 3). The perimeter is 41. Find x and the three sides.

2(x + 6) + 2x − 3 = 41, so 4x + 9 = 41 and x = 8. The sides are 14, 14, and 13.

On a grid.

20. A(1, 1), B(5, 1), C(3, 6). Show that △ ABC is isosceles. Name the congruent sides.

AC = 22 + 52 = 29 and BC = (−2)2 + 52 = 29. AB = 4. So AC ≅ BC.

21. In problem 20, find the midpoint M of AB. Show that CM is perpendicular to AB.

M = (3, 1). CM goes from (3, 6) to (3, 1), so it is vertical. AB is horizontal. Vertical and horizontal lines are perpendicular.

22. A(6, −1), B(1, 0), C(7, 4). Show that AB = AC. Then show that the line from A to the midpoint of BC is perpendicular to BC.

AB = 25 + 1 = 26 and AC = 1 + 25 = 26. M = (4, 2). Slope of AM: 2 − (−1)4 − 6 = −32. Slope of BC: 46 = 23. The product is −1.

Real problems and mistakes.

23. A roof gable is an isosceles triangle. The angle at the peak is 110°. Find each base angle.

(180 − 110) ÷ 2 = 35°

24. A yield sign is an equilateral triangle with sides of 36 inches. Find the perimeter and each angle.

Perimeter: 3 · 36 = 108 inches. Each angle: 60°.

25. Kofi says: “The vertex angle is 40°, so each base angle is 40° too.” Find his mistake.

The base angles are equal to each other, not to the vertex angle. They are (180 − 40) ÷ 2 = 70°.

Challenge

28. An isosceles triangle has a perimeter of 40. The base is 4 more than each leg. Find the three sides. Check that they make a triangle.

Leg = x, base = x + 4. 3x + 4 = 40, so x = 12. The sides are 12, 12, and 16. Check: 12 + 12 = 24 > 16, so it is a triangle.

29. B(0, 0) and C(6, 0) are two vertices of an equilateral triangle. Find the third vertex A above the x-axis. Round the y-coordinate to the nearest tenth. Check with the distance formula.

A is above the midpoint (3, 0), so A = (3, y). AB = 6, so 9 + y2 = 36 and y = 27 ≈ 5.2. A ≈ (3, 5.2).
