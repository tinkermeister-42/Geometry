1.2.1Length on a Number Line

  • A ruler is a number line. To find a length, subtract the two numbers at the ends.
  • For points at a and b: AB = |a − b|
  • A length is never negative, so we use absolute value.
EX 1. A is at −3 and B is at 4. Find AB.

AB = |4 − (−3)| = |7| = 7

EX 2. Find each length.your turn

a. P is at −6 and Q is at 2. PQ = |2 − (−6)| = 8

b. R is at 5 and S is at −1.5. RS = |−1.5 − 5| = 6.5

1.2.3Congruent Segments and Midpoints

  • Segments with the same length are congruent. The symbol is ≅. AB ≅ CD means AB = CD.
  • Use ≅ for figures. Use = for numbers.
  • The midpoint is the point exactly halfway between the endpoints. It makes two congruent segments.
  • A segment bisector is any point, line, ray, or segment that goes through the midpoint.
EX 5. M is the midpoint of AB. AM = 3x + 4 and MB = 5x − 6. Find x and AB.

The two halves are equal: 3x + 4 = 5x − 6

Solve: 10 = 2x,  x = 5

AM = 19   MB = 19   AB = 38

1.2.4Angle Addition and Angle Bisectors

Angle Addition
If D is in the interior of ∠ABC, then m∠ABD + m∠DBC = m∠ABC. An angle bisector is a ray that cuts an angle into two congruent angles.
EX 8. m∠ABD = (2x + 5)°, m∠DBC = (3x − 1)°, m∠ABC = 79°. Find x and each angle.

Equation: (2x + 5) + (3x − 1) = 79

Solve: 5x + 4 = 79,  x = 15

m∠ABD = 35°   m∠DBC = 44°

EX 9. m∠XYW = (x + 20)°, m∠WYZ = (2x + 4)°, m∠XYZ = 90°. Find x and each angle.your turn

3x + 24 = 90, so x = 22.

m∠XYW = 42° and m∠WYZ = 48°.

EX 10. Ray BD bisects ∠ABC. m∠ABD = (4x + 2)° and m∠DBC = (6x − 14)°. Find x and m∠ABC.

The parts are equal: 4x + 2 = 6x − 14

x = 8   Each part: 34°

m∠ABC = 68°

EX 11. Ray QS bisects ∠PQR. m∠PQS = (5x − 7)° and m∠SQR = (3x + 13)°. Find x and m∠PQR.your turn

5x − 7 = 3x + 13, so x = 10.

Each part is 43°, so m∠PQR = 86°.

Reading a protractor. It has an outer scale and an inner scale. Put one side of the angle on a 0. Read the scale that starts at that 0.
EX 12. Find m∠AOB.

Side OA is on the 0 of the inner scale. m∠AOB = 50°

The angle looks small, so it is acute. The outer scale (130°) is the wrong scale.

EX 13. Use the protractor in EX 12. a. Find m∠AOC. b. Find m∠BOC.your turn

a. OC is at 75° on the outer scale, so 105° on the inner scale. m∠AOC = 105°.

b. 50° + m∠BOC = 105°, so m∠BOC = 55°.

Summary
additionbetweenbisectorcongruentmidpointsubtract

1. To find a length on a number line, subtract and use absolute value.

2. AB + BC = AC only when B is between A and C.

3. A midpoint makes two congruent segments.

4. An angle bisector makes two congruent angles.

5. Segments and angles both use addition: the parts add to the whole.

Find the Mistake

20. B is between A and C. AB = 4x − 2, BC = 2x + 8, AC = 42. Diego writes 4x − 2 = 2x + 8 and gets x = 5. What mistake did he make? Find the correct x.

He treated B like a midpoint. B is only between the ends, so the parts add to the whole: (4x − 2) + (2x + 8) = 42, 6x + 6 = 42, x = 6.
