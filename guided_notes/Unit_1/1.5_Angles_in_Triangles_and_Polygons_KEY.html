1.5.1The Triangle Angle Sum

The three angles of a triangle add to 180°. Cut off the corners and put them together: they make a straight line. A line through B parallel to AC shows why: the alternate interior angles are equal.
EX 1. A triangle has angles of 48° and 67°. Find the third angle.

48 + 67 + x = 180, so 115 + x = 180 and x = 65°.

1.5.3Exterior Angles

An exterior angle is outside the triangle, next to an inside angle. The two inside angles far from it are the remote interior angles.

Exterior angle = the sum of the two remote angles

EX 10. In the figure, find the exterior angle.

EX 11. An exterior angle is 130°. One remote angle is 80°. Find the other.your turn

1.5.4Polygons

A polygon is a closed figure made of segments. A regular polygon has all sides equal and all angles equal. A polygon with n sides makes n − 2 triangles. The angles add to (n − 2) · 180°.

EX 14. Find the angle sum of a pentagon and of a decagon.

Decagon: (10 − 2) · 180 = 1440°

EX 15. Find one angle of a regular pentagon and of a regular decagon. (Divide the sum by n.)your turn

540 ÷ 5 = 108°.  1440 ÷ 10 = 144°.

Walk around a polygon. You turn at each corner, and in one trip you turn a full circle. The exterior angles of a convex polygon add to 360°.

Regular polygon: exterior angle = 360 ÷ n. Interior angle = 180 − exterior.

Summary
180°360°baseexteriorremoteregular

1. The angles of a triangle add to 180°.

2. The base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal.

3. An exterior angle equals the sum of the two remote interior angles.

4. The angles of an n-sided polygon add to (n − 2) · 180°. The exterior angles add to 360°.

5. All angles of a regular polygon are equal.

Real Problems

24. A stop sign is a regular octagon. Find each interior angle and each exterior angle.

Exterior: 360 ÷ 8 = 45°. Interior: 180 − 45 = 135°.

25. Esperanza wants to cover a floor with regular pentagon tiles. The corners must meet at a point with no gap. Will it work? (Hint: angles around a point add to 360°.)

No. Each angle is 108°. 3 × 108 = 324 and 4 × 108 = 432. Neither is 360.

Challenge

29. Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 5 times its exterior angle. Find the number of sides.

Interior + exterior = 180, so 6 × exterior = 180 and exterior = 30°. n = 360 ÷ 30 = 12.

30. Use the interior angle sum to explain why the exterior angles of a polygon add to 360°. (Hint: at each corner, interior plus exterior is 180°.)

n corners give n · 180. Take away the interior sum (n − 2) · 180. What is left is 2 · 180 = 360°.
