7.1.1Parts of a Circle

  • A circle is all the points in a plane that are the same distance from one point, the center.
  • We name a circle by its center. Circle P is written ⊙P.
  • All radii of one circle are equal.
  • d = 2r and r = d2
PartWhat it isIn the figure
radiusa segment from the center to a point on the circlePA
chorda segment with both endpoints on the circleEF
diametera chord that goes through the centerBC
arcpart of the circle between two pointsAD
central anglean angle with its vertex at the center∠APD
  • A minor arc is less than half of the circle. It has two letters: AD.
  • A semicircle is exactly half. A major arc is more than half. Both have three letters: BAC and EAF.

7.1.2Circumference and Pi

  • The distance around a circle is its circumference, C.
  • For every circle, C ÷ d is the same number. It is pi, π ≈ 3.14159.

Circumference of a circle
C = π d   or   C = 2 π r. An answer like 12 π is exact. For a decimal, use the π key.

7.1.3Arc Measure and Arc Length

  • Arc measure is in degrees. It equals the central angle.
  • Arc length is a distance, in inches, feet, or meters.
  • A semicircle measures 180°. A major arc measures 360° − its minor arc.
Arc length
arc length = central angle360° · 2 π r

EX 17. Two circles share a center. The inner circle has radius 3. The outer circle has radius 6. Both arcs have a 60° central angle. Find each arc length.

Inner arc AB:

60360 · 2 π(3) = 16 · 6 π = π ≈ 3.14

Outer arc CD:

60360 · 2 π(6) = 16 · 12 π = 2 π ≈ 6.28

Degrees are not a distance
Both arcs measure 60°, but the outer arc is twice as long. Arc measure says how much of the circle. Arc length says how far.

7.1.4Solving with Arcs

The arcs that go all the way around a circle add up to 360°.
EX 18. Find x and each arc.

Write the equation:

2x + (3x + 10) + (x + 20) + (4x − 10) = 360

10x + 20 = 360, 10x = 340, x = 34

The arcs: 68°, 112°, 54°, 126°

EX 19. A circle is cut into four arcs: x°, (2x + 20)°, (3x − 10)°, and (4x)°. Find x and each arc.your turn

x + (2x + 20) + (3x − 10) + 4x = 360, so 10x + 10 = 360 and x = 35.

The arcs are 35°, 90°, 95°, and 140°.

EX 22. Each end of a track is a semicircle with radius 36.5 meters. The two straight sides are 84.4 meters each. How long is one lap? Round to the nearest tenth.

Summary

1. A chord that goes through the center is a diameter.

2. Circumference is C = 2 π r.

3. Arc measure is in degrees. Arc length is a distance.

4. The arcs around a circle add to 360°.

Real Problems

23. A bike wheel has a diameter of 24 inches. How far does it roll in one turn? How many turns does it make in 100 feet? Round to the nearest tenth.

24 π ≈ 75.4 in. 100 ft = 1,200 in. 1,200 ÷ 75.4 ≈ 15.9 turns.

24. A track has two straight sides of 100 meters each. Each end is a semicircle with radius 30 meters. How long is one lap? Round to the nearest tenth.

2 π(30) = 60 π ≈ 188.5. 188.5 + 200 = 388.5 m.

25. A pizza has a diameter of 14 inches. It is cut into 6 equal slices. How long is the crust on one slice? Round to the nearest hundredth.

14 π ÷ 6 = 7 π3 ≈ 7.33 in

26. The minute hand of a clock is 9 inches long. How far does the tip move in 10 minutes? Round to the nearest hundredth.

10 minutes is 16 of a turn (60°). 16 · 18 π = 3 π ≈ 9.42 in

Find the Mistake

27. Samir says: "A circle has a diameter of 10 inches. So C = 2 π(10) = 20 π inches." Find his mistake. What is the correct circumference?

He used the diameter in C = 2 πr. The radius is 5, so C = 2 π(5) = 10 π inches (or C = πd = 10 π).

Challenge

30. In a circle with radius 10 feet, AB is 5 π feet long. Find mAB. Then find the length of chord AB. Hint: draw the radii OA and OB. Round to the nearest hundredth.

The circumference is 20 π. 5 π ÷ 20 π = 14, so mAB = 90°.

The radii make a right triangle with legs 10 and 10. AB = 10 √2 ≈ 14.14 feet.

31. A wheel with a diameter of 26 inches turns 100 times. A bigger wheel with a diameter of 29 inches rolls the same distance. How many times does it turn? Round to the nearest tenth.

Distance: 100 · 26 π = 2,600 π. For the bigger wheel: 29 π n = 2,600 π, so n = 260029 ≈ 89.7 turns.
