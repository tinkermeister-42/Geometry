7.2.1Area of a Circle

  • Cut a circle into thin wedges. Slide them together. The shape is close to a parallelogram.
  • Its base is half the circumference: 12(2 πr) = πr. Its height is r.
  • Area = base · height, so A = πr2.
  • Use the radius, not the diameter. Area is in square units.
EX 2. Find the area of a circle with r = 5 inches. Then with d = 12 feet.

a. A = πr2 = π(5)2 = 25 π ≈ 78.54 square inches

b. r = 6, so A = π(6)2 = 36 π ≈ 113.10 square feet

7.2.2Area of a Sector

  • A sector is the region inside a circle between two radii and their arc.
  • A slice of pizza is a sector.
Area of a sector
sector area = central angle360° · πr2. Arc length uses 2 πr and is a distance. Sector area uses πr2 and is an area.
EX 6. Find the area of each sector. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

a. r = 10 in, central angle 135°

135360 · π(10)2 = 38 · 100 π = 37.5 π ≈ 117.81 in2

b. r = 6 ft, central angle 60°

60360 · π(6)2 = 16 · 36 π = 6 π ≈ 18.85 ft2

EX 7. r = 9 meters and the central angle is 120°. Find the sector area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.your turn

EX 8. A sector of a circle with radius 6 inches has an area of 12 π square inches. Find the central angle.

EX 9. A sector of a circle with radius 4 meters has an area of 6 π square meters. Find the central angle.your turn

EX 10. Find the radius.

a. A 90° sector has an area of 25 π square centimeters.

14 πr2 = 25 π, so r2 = 100 and r = 10 cm

b. A 60° sector has an area of 30 square feet. Round to the nearest hundredth.

16 πr2 = 30, so r2 = 180π and r = √180π ≈ 7.57 ft

EX 11. Find the radius.your turn

a. A 60° sector has an area of 6 π square inches.

16 πr2 = 6 π, so r2 = 36 and r = 6 in

b. A 120° sector has an area of 20 square meters. Round to the nearest tenth.

13 πr2 = 20, so r2 = 60π and r = √60π ≈ 4.4 m

Radius, not diameter, and square units
If you are given the diameter, cut it in half first. Area is in square units. Arc length is in plain units.

7.2.3Shaded Regions and Composite Shapes

To find the area of a shaded region, add or subtract the areas of simple shapes.
EX 12. A ring (an annulus) has an outer radius of 10 feet and an inner radius of 6 feet. Find its area.

Area = outer circle − inner circle

A = π(10)2 − π(6)2 = 100 π − 36 π = 64 π ≈ 201.06 ft2

Do not subtract the radii first
The ring is π(10)2 − π(6)2. It is not π(10 − 6)2. Square each radius first, then subtract.
EX 13. The outer radius is 8 inches and the inner radius is 3 inches. Find the area of the ring. Exact answer and a decimal to the nearest hundredth.your turn

64 π − 9 π = 55 π ≈ 172.79 in2

EX 14. A square has sides of 8 inches. A circle with a diameter of 8 inches is cut out of it. Find the shaded area. Round to the nearest hundredth.

Square: 82 = 64

Circle: r = 4, so A = 16 π

Shaded: 64 − 16 π ≈ 13.73 in2

EX 15. A square has sides of 6 feet. A circle with a diameter of 6 feet is cut out of it. Find the shaded area. Round to the nearest hundredth.your turn

36 − 9 π ≈ 36 − 28.27 = 7.73 ft2

EX 16. A window is a rectangle 4 feet wide and 6 feet tall, with a half circle on top. Find its area. Round to the nearest hundredth.

Rectangle: 4 · 6 = 24

Half circle: r = 2, so 12 π(2)2 = 2 π ≈ 6.28

Window: 24 + 6.28 = 30.28 ft2

EX 17. A window is a rectangle 3 feet wide and 5 feet tall, with a half circle on top. Round to the nearest hundredth.your turn

Rectangle: 15. Half circle: r = 1.5, 12 π(1.5)2 = 1.125 π ≈ 3.53. Total: 18.53 ft2

7.2.4Using Area

EX 18. A 10-inch pizza costs $8. A 14-inch pizza costs $14. Which gives more pizza for each dollar?

10-inch: r = 5, A = 25 π ≈ 78.54. Per dollar: 78.54 ÷ 8 ≈ 9.82

14-inch: r = 7, A = 49 π ≈ 153.94. Per dollar: 153.94 ÷ 14 ≈ 11.00

The better deal is the 14-inch pizza.

EX 19. An 8-inch pizza costs $6. A 12-inch pizza costs $12. Which is the better deal? Round to the nearest hundredth.your turn

8-inch: 16 π ≈ 50.27 in2, and 50.27 ÷ 6 ≈ 8.38 per dollar. 12-inch: 36 π ≈ 113.10, and 113.10 ÷ 12 ≈ 9.42 per dollar. The 12-inch pizza is the better deal.

EX 20. A sprinkler sprays 15 feet and turns through 120°. How much grass does it water? Round to the nearest hundredth.

120360 · π(15)2 = 13 · 225 π = 75 π ≈ 235.62 ft2

EX 21. A sprinkler sprays 12 feet and turns through 150°. Find the watered area. Round to the nearest hundredth.your turn

150360 · π(12)2 = 512 · 144 π = 60 π ≈ 188.50 ft2

EX 22. Fatima says: "A circle has a diameter of 10 inches, so its area is π(10)2 = 100 π." What is wrong?your turn

She used the diameter. The radius is 5, so A = π(5)2 = 25 π.

Summary
πr22 πr360°radiussectorsubtract

1. The area of a circle is A = πr2. Use the radius.

2. A slice of a circle between two radii is a sector. Its area is angle360° of the circle's area.

3. For a ring, subtract the inner circle from the outer circle.

Real Problems

19. A 12-inch pizza costs $9. A 16-inch pizza costs $15. Which is the better deal? Round to the nearest hundredth.

12-inch: 36 π ≈ 113.10, and 113.10 ÷ 9 ≈ 12.57 per dollar. 16-inch: 64 π ≈ 201.06, and 201.06 ÷ 15 ≈ 13.40 per dollar. The 16-inch pizza is the better deal.

20. Daniel paints the top of a round table with a diameter of 6 feet. He puts on 2 coats. A can of paint covers 50 square feet. Is one can enough? Explain.

The top is 9 π ≈ 28.27 ft2. Two coats: 2(28.27) = 56.55 ft2, which is more than 50. No, one can is not enough.

21. A sprinkler sprays 20 feet and turns through 150°. How much grass does it water? Round to the nearest tenth.

150360 · 400 π = 512 · 400 π ≈ 523.6 ft2

22. A round pond has a radius of 10 feet. A path 3 feet wide goes all the way around it. Find the area of the path. Round to the nearest hundredth.

Outer radius 13: 169 π − 100 π = 69 π ≈ 216.77 ft2

Find the Mistake

23. Lucía finds the area of a ring with outer radius 7 and inner radius 4. She writes π(7 − 4)2 = 9 π. Find her mistake. What is the correct area?

She subtracted the radii first. Square each radius, then subtract: 49 π − 16 π = 33 π ≈ 103.67.

27. A circle has radius 3. Another circle has radius 6. Find both areas. How many times as big is the second area? Explain why the answer is not 2.
