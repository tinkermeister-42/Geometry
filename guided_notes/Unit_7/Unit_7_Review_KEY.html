Unit Review - Unit 7: Circles - DHS Geometry
Teacher Key: answers in red

Worked Examples

EX 1. 7.1 A circle has radius 15 feet. Find the length of an arc with central angle 80°. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

80360 · 2 π(15) = 29 · 30 π = 20 π3 ≈ 20.94 ft

EX 2. 7.2 A sector has radius 18 inches and central angle 50°. Find its area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

50360 · π(18)2 = 536 · 324 π = 45 π ≈ 141.37 in2

EX 3. 7.3 Change 330° to radians. Then find the arc length for r = 20 and θ = 2 π5.

330 · π180 = 11 π6.   s = 20 · 2 π5 = 8 π ≈ 25.13

EX 4. 7.4 An inscribed quadrilateral has m∠B = (3x + 9)° and m∠D = (7x − 29)°. Find x and both angles.

(3x + 9) + (7x − 29) = 180, so 10x − 20 = 180 and x = 20. The angles are 69° and 111°.

EX 5. 7.5 Write x2 + y2 − 10x + 4y + 13 = 0 in standard form. Find the center and radius.

(x2 − 10x + 25) + (y2 + 4y + 4) = −13 + 25 + 4, so (x − 5)2 + (y + 2)2 = 16. Center (5, −2), radius 4.

Unit 7 Review - Practice

Practice

7.1 - I can find circumference and arc length, and solve for arcs and radii

1. A circle has radius 7.5 inches. Find its circumference. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

2 π(7.5) = 15 π ≈ 47.12 in

2. A circle has a circumference of 62.8 centimeters. Find its radius. Round to the nearest tenth.

r = 62.82 π ≈ 10.0 cm

3. Find the length of an arc with central angle 100° on a circle with radius 18 meters. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

100360 · 36 π = 10 π ≈ 31.42 m

4. A circle is cut into three arcs measuring 3x°, (5x − 20)°, and (2x + 40)°. Find x and each arc.

10x + 20 = 360, so x = 34. The arcs are 102°, 150°, and 108°.

5. A wheel has a diameter of 28 inches. How far does it roll in 10 turns? Round to the nearest tenth.

10 · 28 π = 280 π ≈ 879.6 in

6. An arc of 120° is 8 π centimeters long. Find the radius.

13(2 πr) = 8 π, so r = 12 cm

7.2 - I can find the area of a circle, a sector, and a shaded region

7. Find the area of a circle with diameter 18 feet. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

r = 9.   A = 81 π ≈ 254.47 ft2

8. A sector has radius 9 meters and central angle 280°. Find its area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

280360 · 81 π = 63 π ≈ 197.92 m2

9. A 40° sector has an area of 10 π square inches. Find the radius. Round to the nearest hundredth.

19 πr2 = 10 π, so r2 = 90 and r = √90 ≈ 9.49 in

10. A ring has an outer radius of 12 and an inner radius of 7. Find its area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

144 π − 49 π = 95 π ≈ 298.45

11. A circle with a diameter of 14 feet is cut out of a square with sides of 14 feet. Find the shaded area. Round to the nearest hundredth.

196 − 49 π ≈ 42.06 ft2

12. A 9-inch pizza costs $7. A 13-inch pizza costs $13. Which is the better deal? Round to the nearest hundredth.

9-inch: 20.25 π ≈ 63.62, and 63.62 ÷ 7 ≈ 9.09 per dollar. 13-inch: 42.25 π ≈ 132.73, and 132.73 ÷ 13 ≈ 10.21 per dollar. The 13-inch pizza is the better deal.

7.3 - I can change between degrees and radians and use s = rθ and A = 12r2θ

13. Change 72° to radians. Give the exact answer.

72 · π180 = 2 π5

14. Change 11 π12 radians to degrees.

11 π12 · 180π = 165°

15. Change 4 radians to degrees. Round to the nearest hundredth.

4 · 180π ≈ 229.18°

16. r = 9 and θ = 5 π6. Find the arc length and the sector area. Give exact answers and decimals rounded to the nearest hundredth.

s = 9 · 5 π6 = 15 π2 ≈ 23.56.   A = 12(81)5 π6 = 135 π4 ≈ 106.03

17. A sector has radius 10 and area 25 π. Find θ in radians and in degrees.

12(100)θ = 25 π, so θ = π2 radians = 90°

7.4 - I can use inscribed angles, inscribed quadrilaterals, and tangent lines

18. An inscribed angle measures 57°. Find its intercepted arc.

2(57°) = 114°

19. An inscribed angle measures (5x − 8)° and its intercepted arc measures (12x − 36)°. Find x and both measures.

5x − 8 = 6x − 18, so x = 10. The angle is 42° and the arc is 84°.

20. AC is a diameter and B is on the circle. m∠A = (6x)° and m∠C = (3x)°. Find x and both angles.

6x + 3x = 90, so x = 10. The angles are 60° and 30°.

21. An inscribed quadrilateral has m∠A = (7x + 8)° and m∠C = (5x + 16)°. Find x and both angles.

12x + 24 = 180, so x = 13. The angles are 99° and 81°.

22. PT is tangent to a circle at T. The radius is 12 and PT = 16. Find OP.

144 + 256 = 400, so OP = 20

23. Two tangent segments from one point measure (6x − 5) and (2x + 19). Find x and the length.

6x − 5 = 2x + 19, so x = 6. Each length is 31.

24. A circle has center (0, 0) and radius 10. A tangent line touches it at T(−6, 8). Find the slope of the radius and the slope of the tangent.

Radius: 8−6 = −43. Tangent: 34.

7.5 - I can write, read, and use the equation of a circle

25. Find the center and radius of (x − 7)2 + (y + 3)2 = 40. Give the radius as a square root and as a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

Center (7, −3). r = √40 ≈ 6.32

26. Write the equation of the circle with center (−5, 2) and radius 6.

(x + 5)2 + (y − 2)2 = 36

27. Write the equation of the circle with center (4, 4) that passes through (10, 12).

r2 = 62 + 82 = 100.   (x − 4)2 + (y − 4)2 = 100

28. A diameter has endpoints (−4, −2) and (8, 6). Write the equation of the circle.

Center (2, 2). r2 = 62 + 42 = 52.   (x − 2)2 + (y − 2)2 = 52

29. Write x2 + y2 + 10x − 6y − 2 = 0 in standard form. Find the center and radius.

(x + 5)2 + (y − 3)2 = 2 + 25 + 9 = 36. Center (−5, 3), radius 6.

30. Is (5, 9) on, inside, or outside the circle (x − 1)2 + (y − 4)2 = 40? Show your work.

42 + 52 = 41, which is more than 40. The point is outside.
