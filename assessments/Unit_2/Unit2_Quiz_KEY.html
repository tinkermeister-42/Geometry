2.2I can use the Pythagorean theorem and its converse.

3. The hypotenuse of a right triangle is 26 and one leg is 10. Find the other leg.

262 − 102 = 576, so the leg is 24

4. A triangle has sides 12, 16, and 21. Is it right, acute, or obtuse? Show your work.

122 + 162 = 400 < 441 = 212. Obtuse.

2.3I can solve real problems with right triangles.

5. An 18 ft ladder has its base 5 ft from a wall. How high up the wall does it reach? Round to the nearest tenth.

324 − 25 = 299 ≈ 17.3 ft

6. A phone screen has a 5.5 in diagonal and is 2.7 in wide. How tall is it? Round to the nearest tenth.

30.25 − 7.29 = 22.96 ≈ 4.8 in

2.4I can find the distance between two points.

8. Find the distance from (3, −1) to (−2, 6). Give the exact answer and round to the nearest tenth.

25 + 49 = 74 ≈ 8.6

2.5I can find a midpoint or a missing endpoint.

9. Find the midpoint of (−8, 3) and (4, −5).

(−2, −1)

B = (−1, −5)

2.6I can use slope to decide parallel or perpendicular, and write equations.

11. Are the lines y = −25x + 1 and 5x − 2y = 8 parallel, perpendicular, or neither? Show the slopes.

Slopes −25 and 52. The product is −1. Perpendicular.

12. Write the equation of the line through (2, −3) that is parallel to y = 4x + 1.

m = 4. −3 = 8 + b, b = −11. y = 4x − 11

2.7I can find the point that divides a segment in a ratio.

13. Find the point 35 of the way from A(−5, −1) to B(5, 9).

Run 10, rise 10. (−5 + 6, −1 + 6) = (1, 5)

14. Find the point that divides the segment from A(2, 8) to B(10, −4) in the ratio 1:3.

k = 14. Run 8, rise −12. (4, 5)

2.8I can find perimeter and area on the coordinate plane.

15. Find the perimeter of the triangle with vertices (0, 1), (5, 1), and (5, 13).

Sides 5, 12, 13. Perimeter 30

16. Use the box method to find the area of the triangle with vertices (1, 2), (8, 4), and (4, 9).

Box 7 · 7 = 49. Corners 7, 10, 10.5. Area = 49 − 27.5 = 21.5

2.9I can check shapes with coordinates.

17. Show that the triangle with vertices A(0, 0), B(6, 3), and C(−2, 4) is a right triangle. Write a "Because" sentence.

Slope of AB is 12. Slope of AC is −2. Because the product is −1, AB ⊥ AC and the triangle is a right triangle.

18. Quadrilateral ABCD has A(1, 1), B(6, 2), C(8, 7), and D(3, 6). What is the most specific name for it? Show your checks and write a "Because" sentence.

AB and DC both have slope 15. AD and BC both have slope 52. It is a parallelogram. The product 15 · 52 = 12, so no right angle. AB = 26 and AD = 29, so not a rhombus. Because it has two pairs of parallel sides and nothing more, it is a parallelogram.
