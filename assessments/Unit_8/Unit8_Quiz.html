Quiz - Unit 8: Area, Surface Area, and Volume - DHS Geometry
NameDatePeriod

8.1I can find the area of polygons and composite figures.

1. A trapezoid has bases of 7 ft and 13 ft and a height of 5 ft. Find its area.

2. A regular hexagon has sides of 10 cm and an apothem of about 8.66 cm. Find its area to the nearest tenth.

8.2I can name solids, find cross sections, and spin shapes into solids.

3. A hexagonal prism has 8 faces and 12 vertices. How many edges does it have?

4. A rectangle 3 in wide and 9 in tall spins about its 9 in side. Name the solid and give its radius and height. What is the cross section if a plane cuts it through the axis?

8.3I can find the surface area of solids.

5. A cylinder has radius 6 ft and height 9 ft. Find its surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

6. A square pyramid has a base side of 8 m and a height of 3 m. Find the slant height, then the surface area.

8.4I can find the volume of prisms and cylinders.

7. A concrete slab is 30 ft by 12 ft and 4 in thick. Find its volume in cubic yards (27 ft3 = 1 yd3). How many whole cubic yards should be ordered?

8. A tank is a cylinder with radius 3 m and height 7 m. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

8.5I can find the volume of pyramids, cones, and spheres.

9. A cone has radius 5 cm and height 12 cm. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

10. A sphere has a diameter of 10 in. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

8.6I can use density and scale to solve real problems.

11. A block is 5 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm and has a mass of 90 g. Find its density. Does it float in water (1 g per cm3)?

12. A model ship is 13 of the size of the real ship. The model has a surface area of 40 ft2 and a volume of 12 ft3. Find the surface area and volume of the real ship.
