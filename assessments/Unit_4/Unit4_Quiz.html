4.1I can name matching parts of congruent figures and use rigid motions.

1. △ JKL ≅ △ PQR. JK = 2x + 5, PQ = 21, m∠L = 4y − 6, and m∠R = 62°. Find x and y.

2. △ ABC has A(−2, 1), B(1, 1), C(−2, 5). Translate it by (x + 4, y − 6). Give the image. Show AC = A′C′.

4.2I can decide which rule shows two triangles are congruent.

3. AB = 6, BC = 10, m∠B = 50°. DE = 6, EF = 10, m∠E = 50°. Which rule shows △ ABC ≅ △ DEF? Write the congruence statement.

4. Two triangles have sides 7 and 9 and a 35° angle that is not between them. Are they always congruent? Explain.

4.3I can use isosceles and equilateral triangles.

5. The base angles of an isosceles triangle are (4x − 6)° and (2x + 14)°. Find x and the vertex angle.

6. A triangle has sides 3x + 1, 5x − 7, and 2x + 5. It is equilateral. Find x and the perimeter.

4.4I can use the properties of quadrilaterals.

7. The diagonals of parallelogram ABCD meet at E. AE = 5x − 2 and EC = 3x + 8. Find x and AC.

8. The diagonals of a rhombus are 18 and 24. Find the side length.

4.5I can make constructions with a compass and a straightedge.

9. Draw a segment 5 cm long. Construct its perpendicular bisector. Label the midpoint M. How long is AM?

10. Draw an angle. Bisect it with a compass and a straightedge. If the angle is 88°, how large is each new angle?
