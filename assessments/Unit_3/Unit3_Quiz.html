3.1I can translate a figure and write a translation rule.

1. Triangle MNP has M(−2, 3), N(1, 5), P(0, −1). Translate it by (x + 6, y − 4). Give the image coordinates.

2. A(5, −3) moves to A′(−1, 2) under a translation. Write the rule and say it in words.

3.2I can reflect a figure and find a line of reflection.

3. Reflect A(−7, 4) over the y-axis. Then reflect A over the line y = −x.

4. B(−1, −2) is reflected to B′(−1, 6). Find the line of reflection. Use the midpoint and the slope.

3.3I can rotate a figure about the origin and find a rotation.

5. Rotate (−6, −1) by 90° counterclockwise and by 180° about the origin.

6. A(5, −3) maps to A′(3, 5) about the origin. Find the rotation. Show that OA = OA′.

3.4I can dilate a figure and find a scale factor.

7. Dilate (8, −6) about the origin with k = 12 and with k = 3.

8. A side of 6 cm becomes 15 cm. Find k. Then dilate A(3, −1) with k = 2 about the center (1, 1).

3.5I can do a sequence of transformations and find symmetry.

9. Reflect (4, 5) over the x-axis. Then translate by (x − 2, y + 3). Find the final image and one rule for both moves.

10. A regular decagon has 10 sides. Give its number of lines of symmetry, its order of rotational symmetry, and its smallest angle of rotation. How many lines of symmetry does a rectangle that is not a square have?
