Unit 6  Quiz

Quiz - Unit 6: Right Triangle Trigonometry - DHS Geometry
NameDatePeriod

6.1I can use the 45-45-90 and 30-60-90 triangle patterns.

1. A 45-45-90 triangle has a hypotenuse of 22 cm. Find each leg. Give the exact answer and the answer to the nearest tenth.

2. A 30-60-90 triangle has a hypotenuse of 26 ft. Find both legs. Give exact answers and round to the nearest tenth.

6.2I can name the sides and use the sine, cosine, and tangent.

3. A right triangle has legs 20 and 21 and hypotenuse 29. For the angle A across from the 20, write sin A, cos A, and tan A as fractions.

4. A line through the origin makes a 50° angle with the x-axis. Find its slope to the nearest thousandth. Is the line steeper or less steep than the line y = x?

6.3I can find a side of a right triangle.

5. An angle is 33° and the adjacent side is 18. Find the opposite side. Round to the nearest tenth.

6. A guy wire is attached 24 feet up a pole and makes a 58° angle with the ground. How long is the wire? Round to the nearest tenth.

6.4I can find an angle of a right triangle.

7. A right triangle has legs 11 and 6. Find the angle across from the 6. Round to the nearest tenth of a degree.

8. A 14-foot ladder reaches 13 feet up a wall. Find the angle the ladder makes with the ground. Round to the nearest tenth of a degree.

6.5I can use sine and cosine of complementary angles.

9. sin 24° = cos θ. Find θ. Then use sin 24° ≈ 0.407 to write cos 66° to the nearest thousandth.

10. tan 35° ≈ 0.700. Use this to find tan 55° to the nearest thousandth.

6.6I can solve problems with angles of elevation and depression.

11. You stand 70 m from a tower. Your eyes are 1.7 m above the ground. The angle of elevation to the top is 52°. How tall is the tower? Round to the nearest tenth.

12. A helicopter is 300 m above the sea. The angle of depression to a ship is 18°. How far is the ship from the point under the helicopter? Round to the nearest tenth.

6.7I can solve real problems with right triangles.

13. A house is 30 feet wide. The roof has a 4/12 pitch and a 1-foot overhang on each side. Find the length of a rafter from the peak to the end of the overhang. Round to the nearest tenth.

14. A hiker walks 12 km on a bearing of 050°. How far north and how far east of the start is she? Round to the nearest tenth.
