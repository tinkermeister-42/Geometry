7.1I can find circumference and arc length.

1. A circle has a diameter of 20 feet. Find its circumference. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

2. A circle has a radius of 24 inches. Find the length of an arc with a central angle of 75°. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

7.2I can find the area of a circle and of a sector.

3. A circle has a diameter of 16 meters. Find its area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

4. A sector has a radius of 16 inches and a central angle of 135°. Find its area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

7.3I can change between degrees and radians and use radian formulas.

5. Change 108° to radians (exact). Then change 7 π9 radians to degrees.

6. A sector has r = 15 centimeters and θ = 2 π3. Find the arc length and the sector area. Give exact answers and decimals rounded to the nearest tenth.

7.4I can use inscribed angles and tangent lines.

7. An inscribed angle measures (3x + 2)°. Its intercepted arc measures (8x − 12)°. Find x, the angle, and the arc.

8. PT is tangent to a circle at T. The radius is 10 and OP = 26. Find PT.

7.5I can write and use the equation of a circle.

9. A diameter has endpoints (−1, 6) and (9, −2). Write the equation of the circle.
