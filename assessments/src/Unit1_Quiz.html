1.2I can use segment and angle addition, midpoints, and bisectors.

3. B is between A and C. AB = 6x − 1, BC = 2x + 9, and AC = 48. Find x, AB, and BC.

{{a}}

8x + 8 = 48, x = 5. AB = 29, BC = 19.

{{/a}}

4. Ray {{ray BD}} bisects ∠ABC. {{mang ABD}} = (3x + 11)° and {{mang DBC}} = (5x − 7)°. Find x and {{mang ABC}}.

{{a}}

3x + 11 = 5x − 7, x = 9. Each part is 38°, so {{mang ABC}} = 76°.

{{/a}}

1.3I can use complementary, supplementary, and vertical angles to find missing angles.

5. Supplementary angles measure (8x + 4)° and (2x + 6)°. Find x and the angles.

{{a}}

10x + 10 = 180, x = 17. The angles are 140° and 40°.

{{/a}}

6. Two lines cross. Vertical angles measure (9x − 14)° and (5x + 18)°. Find x. Then find the measure of one of the other two angles.

{{a}}

9x − 14 = 5x + 18, x = 8. Each is 58°. The others are 180 − 58 = 122°.

{{/a}}

1.4I can use parallel lines and transversals to find angles and to decide if lines are parallel.

7. ℓ ∥ m. Corresponding angles measure (7x − 9)° and (4x + 21)°. Find x and the angles.

{{a}}

7x − 9 = 4x + 21, x = 10. Each angle is 61°.

{{/a}}

8. A transversal makes alternate interior angles of 72° and 108°. Are the two lines parallel? Explain.

{{a}}

No. Alternate interior angles must be equal for parallel lines, and 72 ≠ 108.

{{/a}}

1.5I can find angles in triangles and polygons.

9. The angles of a triangle are (x + 8)°, (3x − 2)°, and (2x + 6)°. Find x and each angle.

{{a}}

6x + 12 = 180, x = 28. The angles are 36°, 82°, and 62°.

{{/a}}

10. A regular polygon has an interior angle of 162°. How many sides does it have? Show your work.

{{a}}

Exterior angle: 180 − 162 = 18°. 360 ÷ 18 = 20 sides.

{{/a}}
