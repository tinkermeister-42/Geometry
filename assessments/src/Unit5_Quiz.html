1. Solve {{f 3/x − 1}} = {{f 9/15}}.

{{a}}

{{/a}}

2. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 1 : 3 : 5. Find each angle.

{{a}}

9x = 180, so x = 20. The angles are 20°, 60°, and 100°.

3. △ ABC ∼ △ DEF. AB = 12, DE = 18, and EF = 21. Find BC.

{{a}}

4. Two similar triangles have scale factor 3 from the small one to the large one. The small triangle has perimeter 11 and area 7. Find the perimeter and area of the large triangle.

{{a}}

Perimeter: 3 · 11 = 33. Area: 32 · 7 = 63.

5.5I can use similar triangles to measure heights and distances.

9. A person 1.8 m tall has a shadow 1.2 m long. A tree casts a shadow 8 m long at the same time. How tall is the tree?

{{a}}

{{f 1.8/1.2}} = {{f h/8}}, so 1.2h = 14.4 and h = 12 m.

{{/a}}

10. A lamp is 12 ft tall. Daniel is 6 ft tall and stands 8 ft from the lamp. How long is his shadow? Let s be the shadow length.

{{a}}

The big triangle has base 8 + s. {{f 12/8 + s}} = {{f 6/s}}, so 12s = 6(8 + s). 12s = 48 + 6s, so 6s = 48 and s = 8 ft.

{{/a}}
