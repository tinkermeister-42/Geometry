Teacher Key: answers in red

5.1I can write and solve proportions, use scales, and split a total in a ratio.

1. Solve 3x − 1 = 915.

9(x − 1) = 45, so x − 1 = 5 and x = 6.

2. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 1 : 3 : 5. Find each angle.

9x = 180, so x = 20. The angles are 20°, 60°, and 100°.

5.2I can decide whether figures are similar and use the scale factor.

3. △ ABC ∼ △ DEF. AB = 12, DE = 18, and EF = 21. Find BC.

k = 1812 = 1.5. BC = 211.5 = 14.

4. Two similar triangles have scale factor 3 from the small one to the large one. The small triangle has perimeter 11 and area 7. Find the perimeter and area of the large triangle.

Perimeter: 3 · 11 = 33. Area: 32 · 7 = 63.

5.3I can decide whether two triangles are similar and find missing sides.

5. Triangle 1 has sides 5, 7, and 9. Triangle 2 has sides 15, 21, and 27. Are they similar? Which rule shows it? What is k?

155 = 217 = 279 = 3. Yes, SSS, with k = 3.

6. DE ∥ BC. AD = 4, DB = 8, and DE = 5. Find BC.

AB = 12. 412 = 5BC, so 4 · BC = 60 and BC = 15.

5.4I can use parallel lines and midsegments in triangles.

7. DE ∥ BC. AD = x, DB = 9, AE = 8, and EC = 12. Find x.

x9 = 812, so 12x = 72 and x = 6.

8. A(0, 0), B(8, 4), C(4, 10). M is the midpoint of AB and N is the midpoint of BC. Show that MN ∥ AC.

M = (4, 2) and N = (6, 7). Slope of MN: 7 − 26 − 4 = 52. Slope of AC: 104 = 52. Equal slopes, so parallel.

5.5I can use similar triangles to measure heights and distances.

9. A person 1.8 m tall has a shadow 1.2 m long. A tree casts a shadow 8 m long at the same time. How tall is the tree?

1.81.2 = h8, so 1.2h = 14.4 and h = 12 m.

10. A lamp is 12 ft tall. Daniel is 6 ft tall and stands 8 ft from the lamp. How long is his shadow? Let s be the shadow length.

The big triangle has base 8 + s. 128 + s = 6s, so 12s = 6(8 + s). 12s = 48 + 6s, so 6s = 48 and s = 8 ft.
