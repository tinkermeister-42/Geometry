# Welcome to Geometry {.unnumbered}

Welcome to Geometry at Frederick Douglass High School!

A builder makes a perfect square corner with three numbers: 3, 4, and 5.
A wheelchair ramp must rise 1 inch for every 12 inches it goes forward.
You can find the height of a tree from the length of its shadow.

All of these are geometry. Geometry is the math of shape, size, and position.
In this class you will measure things you cannot reach, check if a shape is what it looks like, and use algebra to answer questions about pictures.

***

## 🧭 How This Book Works

Each lesson has:

- Examples we work together in class
- Practice problems to do on your own
- A printable **guided notes** sheet with the same examples and practice

Many lessons use the **coordinate plane**. The tools you learn in Unit 2 (distance, midpoint, and slope) come back in every unit after it.
